# Websites Tracking Global Climate Change Statistics

A curated list of authoritative sources for global climate indicators, temperature records, greenhouse gas data, sea-level rise, ice extent, and related statistics.

## Official Dashboards & Observational Records

- **[NOAA Climate.gov – Global Climate Dashboard](https://www.climate.gov/climatedashboard)**  
  U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration interactive dashboard tracking key indicators: greenhouse gases, Arctic sea ice, atmospheric CO₂, mountain glaciers, ocean heat, sea level, snow cover, and surface temperature.

- **[Indicators of Global Climate Change (IGCC)](https://www.igccclimate.org/)**  
  Annual updates of key IPCC-consistent climate indicators (human-induced warming, emissions, carbon budget, energy imbalance, etc.) produced by an international team of scientists. Includes dashboards and peer-reviewed papers.

- **[Climate Change Tracker](https://climatechangetracker.org/global)**  
  Real-time / monthly-updated tracking of observed global warming, atmospheric CO₂ and CH₄ levels, emissions, remaining carbon budget, and sea levels, aligned with IPCC AR6 methods.

## Comprehensive Data Portals & Visualizations

- **[Our World in Data – Climate Change](https://ourworldindata.org/climate-change)**  
  Extensive collection of interactive charts and downloadable datasets on temperature anomalies, sea level, sea ice, ice sheets, ocean heat, greenhouse gas concentrations, and more.

- **[Climate Watch (World Resources Institute)](https://www.climatewatchdata.org)**  
  Open data platform for historical GHG emissions (160+ years, 196 countries), NDCs, net-zero targets, pathways, and policy analysis.

- **[IPCC Data Distribution Centre – Observational Record](https://www.ipcc-data.org/observ/index.html)**  
  Access to observed climate system data, atmospheric composition, and related datasets used in IPCC assessments.

## Additional Key Sources

- **[Global Carbon Project](https://www.globalcarbonproject.org/)**  
  Authoritative annual Global Carbon Budget reports and data on CO₂ emissions from fossil fuels and land-use change.

- **[EDGAR – Emissions Database for Global Atmospheric Research](https://edgar.jrc.ec.europa.eu/)**  
  European Commission Joint Research Centre database of anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions by country and sector.
