Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)

Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is an American multinational semiconductor company that designs and produces computer processors, graphics processing units (GPUs), and related technologies for consumer, enterprise, and data-center markets.

AMD was founded in 1969 by a group of former Fairchild Semiconductor executives, including Jerry Sanders. For much of its history it competed primarily with Intel in the x86 CPU market. In the 2010s and 2020s AMD regained significant market share with its Zen-architecture Ryzen and EPYC processors and expanded its GPU business (Radeon) and data-center accelerators.

The company is headquartered in Santa Clara, California, and is one of the major players in high-performance computing, gaming, and AI hardware.
