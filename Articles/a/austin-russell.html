Austin Russell

Known for: Founder and CEO of Luminar Technologies (lidar for autonomous vehicles)
Programs: Thiel Fellow (2013)

Austin Russell founded Luminar, a company building lidar sensors and software for autonomous driving and related applications. As a teenager he worked on sensing hardware and later took Luminar public; at peak valuations he was widely reported as among the world’s youngest self-made billionaires. He is a 2013 Thiel Fellow and appears on FuturPedia’s selected Thiel alumni and Corporate Thug Chads rosters.
