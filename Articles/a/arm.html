Arm Holdings (ARM)

Arm Holdings (commonly known as Arm or ARM) is a British semiconductor and software design company. It is best known for designing the ARM architecture — a family of reduced-instruction-set computing (RISC) processor architectures widely used in mobile devices, embedded systems, and increasingly in servers, PCs, and automotive applications.

The company originated from Acorn Computers in the 1980s. The first ARM processor was developed in the mid-1980s. Arm licenses its architecture and designs to other semiconductor companies rather than manufacturing chips itself. Major licensees include Apple, Qualcomm, Samsung, NVIDIA, and many others.

Arm processors power the majority of the world’s smartphones and a large share of other electronic devices. The company has been owned by SoftBank Group since 2016 (with a period of planned acquisition by NVIDIA that did not close).
