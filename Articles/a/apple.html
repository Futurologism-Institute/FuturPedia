Apple Inc.

Apple Inc. is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. It designs, develops, and sells consumer electronics, software, and online services.

The company was founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as Apple Computer Company. Its early products included the Apple I and Apple II personal computers. Landmark later products include the Macintosh, iPod, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac computers, along with software platforms such as macOS, iOS, and services including the App Store, iCloud, and Apple Music.

Apple is one of the world’s largest technology companies by revenue and market capitalization and is known for its emphasis on industrial design, integrated hardware–software ecosystems, and retail presence.
