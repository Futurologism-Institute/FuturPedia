History of Australia

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Indigenous & Colonial

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have lived on the continent for tens of thousands of years. British colonization began with the First Fleet in 1788. Separate colonies developed and later federated.

Federation & After

The Commonwealth of Australia was formed in 1901. The country fought in both world wars, expanded its welfare state and immigration, and evolved from a British dominion into an independent nation within the Commonwealth, with ongoing debates about republic status and Indigenous recognition.
