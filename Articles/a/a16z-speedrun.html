a16z Speedrun

Operator: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)
Launched: 2023
Type: Early-stage accelerator (pre-seed / seed)
Program: ~12 weeks, in-person (primarily San Francisco)
Overview for FuturPedia reference. Speedrun began with a games/entertainment focus and expanded into a broader horizontal program including AI and consumer tech.

a16z Speedrun is Andreessen Horowitz’s accelerator for very early-stage startups. Cohorts are highly selective (reported acceptance rates well under 1%). Accepted companies typically receive substantial capital (up to about $1M in program terms), large partner credit packages (cloud, AI models, tooling), operator support, and a Demo Day in front of investors.

The program has grown quickly since 2023, with multiple cohorts (SR001, SR002, …), hundreds of startups funded, and a founder community reported in the hundreds.
