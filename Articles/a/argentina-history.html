History of Argentina

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Colonial & Independence

Spanish colonial rule centered on the Río de la Plata. Independence movements in the early 19th century led to the formation of Argentina after prolonged conflict and internal struggles.

Modern Argentina

The country experienced export-led growth, mass immigration, the rise of Peronism under Juan Domingo Perón, military interventions, the Dirty War, the Falklands/Malvinas conflict, and a return to democracy. Argentina remains a major Latin American economy and cultural force.
