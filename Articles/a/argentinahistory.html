History of Argentina (Structural Outline)

Pre-Colonial & Colonial
Indigenous peoples (Mapuche, Guaraní, Diaguita and others), Spanish colonization, Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata.
Independence & Nation-Building
May Revolution (1810), wars of independence, civil wars between unitarios and federales, Constitution of 1853, consolidation of the interior.
Modern Era
Great immigration wave, agro-export boom, Radical and Conservative periods, Peronism, military interventions.
Contemporary
Return to democracy (1983), economic crises and recoveries, Kirchnerism and alternating political cycles, ongoing institutional development.
