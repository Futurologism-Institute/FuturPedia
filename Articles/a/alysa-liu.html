Alysa Liu

Born: 8 August 2005 (Clovis, California)
Sport: Figure skating (women’s singles)
Notable: 2026 Olympic champion (women’s singles and team); 2025 World champion; two-time U.S. national champion

Alysa Liu is an American figure skater. At 13 she became the youngest U.S. women’s national champion (2019) and repeated in 2020. She was among the first American women to land a quadruple jump in international competition and combined quads and triple Axels in landmark junior programs.

After competing at the 2022 Winter Olympics and winning world bronze that year, she retired, then returned and won the 2025 World Championships—the first U.S. woman to do so since 2006. At the 2026 Winter Olympics she won gold in the team event and in women’s singles, the first U.S. women’s singles Olympic gold since Sarah Hughes in 2002.
