Alchemy (Crypto Infrastructure Platform)

Alchemy is a blockchain developer platform that provides infrastructure, APIs, and tools for building and scaling decentralized applications. It is often described as a leading “node-as-a-service” and Web3 development platform.

The company was founded in 2017 and is headquartered in San Francisco. Alchemy offers enhanced blockchain APIs, developer tools, monitoring, and support for multiple networks including Ethereum and other major chains. Its customers have included a wide range of crypto projects, fintech companies, and institutions.

Founders

  • Nikil Viswanathan – Co-Founder and CEO. Stanford Computer Science graduate (BS & MS). Previously worked on products at Google, Microsoft, and Facebook.
  • Joe Lau – Co-Founder and President (formerly CTO). Also a Stanford Computer Science graduate (BS & MS). Focuses on technical infrastructure and later on institutional and fintech adoption of blockchain.

Before Alchemy, Viswanathan and Lau collaborated on earlier projects, including the social app Down to Lunch. They pivoted into crypto infrastructure after encountering the difficulty of building reliable blockchain applications in 2017.
