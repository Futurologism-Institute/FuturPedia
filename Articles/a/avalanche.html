Avalanche (AVAX)

Avalanche is a high-throughput smart-contract platform designed for decentralized applications and enterprise use cases. It was developed by Ava Labs and launched in 2020.

Avalanche uses a novel consensus protocol that enables fast finality and high transaction throughput while maintaining decentralization. The network consists of multiple specialized blockchains (including the Exchange Chain, Platform Chain, and Contract Chain) that can operate in parallel.

Its native token is AVAX, used for staking, transaction fees, and subnet creation. Avalanche supports the creation of custom blockchains (subnets) tailored to specific applications.
