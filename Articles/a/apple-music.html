Apple Music

Launched: 2015
Operator: Apple Inc.
Type: Subscription audio streaming (music, radio, spatial audio, and related)

Apple Music is Apple’s subscription streaming service for music and related audio. It succeeded earlier efforts such as iTunes Store downloads as the center of Apple’s music offering and competes with Spotify, YouTube Music, and others.

Features have included curated playlists, algorithmic radio-style stations, spatial audio, and integration with Apple devices and the broader Apple account ecosystem. Discovery tools (For You, radio, autoplay) provide a form of continuous or “random-adjacent” listening within licensed catalogues—not open web link roulette.
