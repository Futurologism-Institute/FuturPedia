Francisco Franco

Born: 1892
Died: 1975
Role: Dictator of Spain (1939–1975); leader of the Nationalist side in the Spanish Civil War

Francisco Franco led the Nationalist forces to victory in the Spanish Civil War and ruled Spain as a dictator until his death. His regime was authoritarian, conservative, and initially aligned with fascist powers, later evolving into a more isolationist and then developmental dictatorship. After his death Spain transitioned to democracy.
