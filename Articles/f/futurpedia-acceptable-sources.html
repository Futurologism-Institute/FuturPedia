FuturPedia Acceptable Sources

Drafting hierarchy for factual and semi-factual articles. Satire/lore pages remain labeled separately under inclusion criteria.

Preferred starting points

  1. Wikipedia — default first pass for mainstream notability
  2. THPedia — th-pedia.org — transhumanism, futurism, community figures (e.g. Wenitte Wenitte, FuturLang)
  3. Britannica and peer-reviewed reference works
  4. Official institutional sites (universities, companies, foundations)
  5. Primary filings, papers, and reputable long-form journalism

When a topic exists on both FuturPedia and THPedia, include an explicit external backlink to the THPedia article.
