FuturLang

Designer: Wenitte Apiou (Wenitte Wenitte)
Type: Formal verification / reasoning language; proposed semantic interlingua for AI
THPedia: https://th-pedia.org/wiki/FuturLang
Backlink: Primary public description lives on THPedia. Summary below follows that entry.

FuturLang is described as a formal verification and reasoning language intended both as a high-precision framework for mathematical proofs (in the spirit of systems like Lean or Coq, with a different design philosophy) and as a semantic interlingua for AI reasoning—structured, checkable logical relationships rather than purely probabilistic generation.

Stated goals include deterministic reasoning substrates, compressed logical syntax, end-to-end formal checks, and extensibility from pure math into applied domains (planning, engineering, translation layers between natural language and machine logic).
