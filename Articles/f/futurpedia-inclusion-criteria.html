FuturPedia Inclusion Criteria

Editorial standards for what belongs in FuturPedia, at what depth, and under what labels.
Doctrine
FuturPedia documents reality carefully, programs selectively, and lore explicitly—and never confuses the three.

1. Core mission filter

Include if the topic helps a reader understand:

  • How the world works (science, institutions, history, technology)
  • Who builds power, capital, culture, or knowledge
  • Ideas, programs, and people that shape futures (startups, research, politics, media)

Default posture: encyclopedic + slightly opinionated formalization—not a pure fan wiki and not a pure news dump.

2. Tiered inclusion

TierWhat belongsDepth
A — Canon Countries, major universities, foundational science, major historical periods, core institutions (e.g. YC, Thiel Fellowship, a16z Speedrun, Olympics) Full overview + timeline or alumni where useful
B — Notables Clearly notable people, companies, movements with verifiable public impact Short bio / selected list entry
C — Lore / satire Corporate Thug Chads, memes, casting jokes, playful “formalizations” Explicitly labeled satirical; link to straight entry when one exists
D — Experimental Edge topics, speculative frames, thin sources Sandbox only; easy to delete or merge later

Anything in C must never be presented as fact-equivalent to A/B.

3. Concrete inclusion criteria

People

  • Public role with durable footprint (founder, head of state, major scientist, cultural figure), or
  • Clearly notable within a named program (YC / Thiel / Speedrun at “selected notables” level), or
  • Explicit lore/satire with a disclaimer

Avoid (unless satire and labeled): private individuals with no public record, minors, pure gossip, unverifiable claims.

Institutions & programs

  • Real organizations with public presence and lasting relevance
  • Prefer primary structure: overview → alumni/roster → related figures

History & countries

  • Standard high-level arcs are enough unless a reader asks for depth
  • Prefer accuracy over completeness; mark “selected timeline” when appropriate

Science / entertainment / sports

  • Concepts and fields a general educated reader might look up
  • Figures only when load-bearing for the field or already cross-linked

4. Style & labeling rules

  • Straight articles: neutral, compact tone; “FuturPedia formalization”
  • Satire: banner note + link to non-satire page when possible
  • Selected lists: always state selected, not exhaustive (especially YC, Thiel Fellowship, a16z Speedrun)
  • Fictional / house characters: keep consistent; never mix into factual bios without clear fiction framing

5. Quality bar before push

  1. Is there a clear reader use-case?
  2. Does it fit an existing folder letter / category?
  3. Can it cross-link to something already in the tree?
  4. If satirical, is that obvious in the first screen?
  5. Would you still want this page in a year?

If three or more answers are “no,” skip or park as experimental.

6. Priority guidance

Higher priority

  • Index / hub pages (Universities, Fellowships, History by region, Entertainment)
  • Missing straight bios for people who currently only appear in satire lists
  • Deeper Tier A content (countries, science clusters, institutions)
  • Consistency pass (shared HTML shell, shared disclaimer language)

Lower priority

  • Endless alumni scraping
  • Duplicate roster mirrors
  • Thin one-line pages with no links
This page is itself a living editorial document. Update it when practice diverges from policy.
