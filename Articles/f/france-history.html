History of France

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Medieval & Early Modern

From the Frankish kingdoms and the Capetian monarchy through the Hundred Years’ War, the rise of absolutism under Louis XIV, and the cultural and political centrality of France in Europe.

Revolution & 19th Century

The French Revolution (1789), Napoleonic era, successive monarchies and republics, and the construction of a modern nation-state and colonial empire.

20th Century & After

World Wars, the Fourth and Fifth Republics, decolonization, and France’s role as a major European and global power. The Fifth Republic, established under Charles de Gaulle in 1958, remains the current constitutional framework.
