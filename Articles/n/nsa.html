National Security Agency (NSA)

The National Security Agency / Central Security Service (NSA/CSS) is a U.S. government agency responsible for foreign signals intelligence (SIGINT) and cybersecurity for national security systems.

It was established in 1952 by a directive from President Harry Truman, consolidating cryptologic efforts that had proven critical during World War II.

NSA’s stated missions include providing foreign signals intelligence to U.S. policymakers and military forces, and protecting U.S. national security systems through cybersecurity efforts.

Source: Official NSA public materials.
