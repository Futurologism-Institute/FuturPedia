Nigerian Universities

Nigeria has one of the largest higher-education systems in Africa, with federal, state, and private universities.

Major Federal Universities (selected)

  • University of Ibadan (Ibadan) – founded 1948; Nigeria’s oldest university
  • University of Nigeria, Nsukka (Nsukka) – founded 1960
  • Ahmadu Bello University (Zaria) – founded 1962
  • University of Lagos (Lagos) – founded 1962
  • Obafemi Awolowo University (Ile-Ife) – founded 1962
  • University of Benin (Benin City)
  • University of Ilorin (Ilorin)
  • University of Jos (Jos)
  • University of Port Harcourt (Port Harcourt)
  • Federal University of Technology, Akure and other specialized federal universities of technology

Notes

In addition to federal universities, each state operates one or more state universities, and a growing number of private universities have been established since the 1990s. The National Universities Commission (NUC) is the regulatory body overseeing accreditation and standards.

Sources: Public higher-education records.
