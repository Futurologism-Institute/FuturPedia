History of Nigeria

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-colonial Period

Nigeria’s territory contained diverse societies and states, including the Hausa city-states and later the Sokoto Caliphate in the north, the Yoruba states (including Oyo) in the southwest, and the Benin Kingdom and Igbo communities in the south and east.

Colonial Period

Britain gradually established control in the 19th and early 20th centuries, creating the Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria. The north and south were administered differently before fuller amalgamation.

Independence and Post-Independence

Nigeria gained independence on 1 October 1960. The First Republic was followed by military coups, the Biafran War (1967–1970), successive military regimes, and returns to civilian rule. Oil wealth has shaped politics and the economy. The Fourth Republic began in 1999 and has continued under multiparty elections, though challenges of governance, security, and development remain significant.
