The Neopolis Theory of Government, described by Wenitte Apiou on December 30, 2022, in a since-deleted blog post, envisions a decentralized, interconnected network of autonomous city-states as the foundation for global governance.[1] Inspired by the Ancient Greek city-state model, Neopolis emphasizes the diversity of Earth’s ecosystems, cultures, and governance styles—referred to as “Terrediversity.” Each city-state operates independently, creating its own laws and policies tailored to local needs, while federations of city-states collaborate globally on trade, resource-sharing, and scientific advancement. Rejecting the concept of a single unified world government, Neopolis promotes local autonomy, cultural preservation, and innovation while fostering international cooperation. This model sees the world as a tapestry of diverse, interconnected societies that thrive through mutual respect and shared goals. Wenitte Apiou. "The Neopolis Theory of Government".
