Nelson Mandela

Born: 18 July 1918
Died: 5 December 2013
Role: Anti-apartheid leader; first President of democratic South Africa (1994–1999); Nobel Peace Prize laureate

Nelson Mandela was a central leader of the African National Congress and the struggle against apartheid. He spent 27 years in prison (1962–1990). After his release he played a decisive role in negotiating the transition to majority rule.

Elected president in South Africa’s first democratic elections in 1994, he emphasized national reconciliation. He is widely regarded as one of the most significant political and moral figures of the 20th century.
