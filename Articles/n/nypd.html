New York City Police Department (NYPD)

The New York City Police Department (NYPD) is the primary law enforcement agency of New York City. It is one of the oldest and the largest municipal police departments in the United States.

A professional police force was established in 1845, replacing the earlier night-watch system. In 1898, with the consolidation of the five boroughs into Greater New York, the various local police forces were merged into a single department.

The NYPD is responsible for law enforcement, public safety, and crime prevention within New York City.
