Cory Levy

Role: Founder of Z Fellows; early-stage investor
Earlier: Co-founder of After School (teen social network; later acquihire path); FirstText; high-school internships at firms including Founders Fund and Union Square Ventures

Cory Levy is an entrepreneur and early-stage investor best known as the founder of Z Fellows, a one-week program that gives technical builders a cash grant and access to Silicon Valley mentors. He has described the program as putting “first believer” capital and relationships in front of undiscovered founders.

As a teenager and young founder he co-founded After School, a teen social product that reached large scale and was later associated with an acquihire path. He studied computer science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and left without completing a conventional degree path. He has been listed on Forbes 30 Under 30 (consumer technology / dorm-room founders categories in public profiles) and is an active angel and pre-seed investor alongside running Z Fellows.
