History of China

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference. Chinese history spans millennia.

Imperial China

Successive dynasties (including Qin, Han, Tang, Song, Ming, and Qing) developed bureaucratic governance, Confucian ideology, major technological and cultural achievements, and a long tradition of unified empire punctuated by periods of division.

Late Qing & Republican Period

The 19th and early 20th centuries saw internal crises, foreign pressure, the fall of the Qing dynasty (1911), the Republic of China, warlordism, and conflict with Japan.

People’s Republic of China

The Communist Party under Mao Zedong established the People’s Republic in 1949. Major campaigns included land reform, the Great Leap Forward, and the Cultural Revolution. From the late 1970s, reforms under Deng Xiaoping opened the economy and produced rapid growth. China has since become a major global economic and political power.
