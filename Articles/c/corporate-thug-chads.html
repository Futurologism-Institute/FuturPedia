Category: Corporate Thug Chads
Definition (FuturPedia satirical formalization): Figures who move through capital, code, or power with main-character calm — boardroom gravity optional, aura mandatory.
Satirical FuturPedia category. Not a ranking, endorsement, or factual organizational membership. Thiel Fellow section lists selected male fellows (notable + recent cohorts); the full historical fellowship exceeds 300 people and is not exhaustively reproduced here.

The Corporate Thug Chad is not necessarily a founder who yells. He is the one the room tilts toward — tech operator, investor, dynastic soft power, or political apex predator. Credentials vary. Presence does not.
