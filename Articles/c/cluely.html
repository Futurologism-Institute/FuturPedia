Cluely

Founded: 2025
Founders: Roy Lee (CEO), Neel Shanmugam, Alex Chen
Product: Real-time AI meeting / interview assistance (desktop overlay)

Cluely is an AI startup whose product monitors screen and audio during virtual meetings and interviews to provide live assistance. It grew out of Interview Coder, a tool associated with helping candidates during technical coding interviews. Founders Roy Lee and Neel Shanmugam were suspended from Columbia University in connection with that earlier tool, then launched Cluely with viral “cheat on everything” marketing before positioning the product more broadly as a meeting assistant.

The company raised a seed round (reported ~$5.3M) and a Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz (reported ~$15M). It has been highly controversial over ethics, detection by proctoring systems, and founder marketing tactics.
