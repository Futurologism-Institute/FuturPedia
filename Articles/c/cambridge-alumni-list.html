University of Cambridge — Notable Alumni List

Selected list of notable Cambridge alumni and former students for FuturPedia reference. Not exhaustive.

Science & Mathematics

  • Isaac Newton — Physicist, mathematician, author of Principia
  • Charles Darwin — Naturalist, theory of evolution
  • Alan Turing — Mathematician, computer science pioneer, codebreaker
  • Stephen Hawking — Theoretical physicist and cosmologist
  • James Clerk Maxwell — Physicist (electromagnetism)
  • Ernest Rutherford — “Father of nuclear physics”
  • Francis Crick & James Watson — Structure of DNA
  • Demis Hassabis — Co-founder of DeepMind, Nobel Prize

Politics & Leadership

  • Oliver Cromwell — Lord Protector
  • King Charles III
  • Manmohan Singh — Former Prime Minister of India

  • Stephen Fry — Actor, writer, comedian
  • Hugh Laurie — Actor

  • Rachel Weisz — Actress
  • Tom Hiddleston — Actor
  • Sacha Baron Cohen — Actor and comedian
