Cardano (ADA)

Cardano is a proof-of-stake blockchain platform focused on security, scalability, and sustainability. It was founded by Charles Hoskinson (a co-founder of Ethereum) and launched in 2017.

Cardano is developed with a research-driven approach, emphasizing peer-reviewed academic papers and formal methods. Its native cryptocurrency is ADA. The platform aims to support smart contracts, decentralized applications, and identity solutions, with particular attention to use cases in developing regions.

Cardano uses a layered architecture and the Ouroboros proof-of-stake consensus protocol.
