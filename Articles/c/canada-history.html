History of Canada

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Indigenous & Colonial

Indigenous peoples inhabited the territory for millennia. French and British colonization created competing claims; Britain ultimately gained control after the Seven Years’ War. The creation of the Dominion of Canada in 1867 under Confederation began the modern federal state.

Modern Canada

Expansion westward, two world wars, the development of the welfare state, bilingualism and multiculturalism, and ongoing relations with Indigenous peoples shape modern Canada. It remains a constitutional monarchy and federal democracy within the Commonwealth.
