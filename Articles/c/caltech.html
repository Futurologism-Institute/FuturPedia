California Institute of Technology (Caltech)

Founded: 1891
Location: Pasadena, California
Type: Private research university

Caltech is a small but highly influential research university specializing in science and engineering. It manages the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) for NASA and has produced many Nobel laureates.

Notable in FuturPedia contexts are Phoebe Wenitte and Wenitte Wenitte, Professors of Mathematics and Electrical Engineering.
