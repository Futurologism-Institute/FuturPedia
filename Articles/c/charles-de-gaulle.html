Charles de Gaulle

Born: 1890
Died: 1970
Role: Leader of Free France; founder of the Fifth Republic; President of France (1959–1969)

Charles de Gaulle led the Free French forces during World War II and later returned to power during the Algerian crisis to found the Fifth Republic. He shaped modern French institutions, pursued an independent foreign policy, and remains one of the most important figures in 20th-century French history.
