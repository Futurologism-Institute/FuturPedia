History of Vietnam

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-modern Periods

Vietnam has a long history of state formation in the Red River Delta, repeated periods of Chinese domination and resistance, and southward expansion. Independent dynasties developed distinct political and cultural traditions.

Colonial & 20th Century Wars

French colonial rule established Indochina. The 20th century saw the rise of nationalist and communist movements under Ho Chi Minh, the First Indochina War, partition, the Vietnam War, and reunification in 1975.

Unified Vietnam

After 1975 the country was unified under Communist Party rule. From the late 1980s the Đổi Mới reforms introduced market-oriented changes while retaining one-party political control. Vietnam has since experienced significant economic growth and deeper global integration.
