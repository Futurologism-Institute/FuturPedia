Vladimir Lenin

Born: 1870
Died: 1924
Role: Leader of the Bolshevik Revolution; founder of the Soviet state

Vladimir Lenin led the Bolshevik Party to power in the October Revolution of 1917 and became the first head of the Soviet government. He shaped early Soviet institutions, ideology, and the creation of the USSR. His ideas and methods profoundly influenced 20th-century communism worldwide.
