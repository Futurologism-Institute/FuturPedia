Haile Selassie

Born: 1892
Died: 1975
Role: Emperor of Ethiopia (1930–1974)

Haile Selassie was the last emperor of Ethiopia. He resisted the Italian invasion of the 1930s, addressed the League of Nations, and later became a symbol of African independence and unity. He was a founding figure in the Organization of African Unity. His long reign ended with the 1974 revolution. He is also a central figure in the Rastafari movement.
