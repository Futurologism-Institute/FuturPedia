Harvard University — Notable Alumni List

This is a selected list of notable Harvard University alumni and former students for FuturPedia reference. It is not exhaustive. Harvard has produced an exceptionally large number of leaders across every major field.

U.S. Presidents

  • John Adams — 2nd President
  • John Quincy Adams — 6th President
  • Rutherford B. Hayes — 19th President
  • Theodore Roosevelt — 26th President
  • Franklin D. Roosevelt — 32nd President
  • John F. Kennedy — 35th President
  • George W. Bush — 43rd President (MBA)
  • Barack Obama — 44th President (J.D., Harvard Law)

Technology & Business

  • Bill Gates — Co-founder of Microsoft (attended)
  • Mark Zuckerberg — Founder of Facebook / Meta (attended)
  • Sheryl Sandberg — Former COO of Meta (MBA)
  • Michael Bloomberg — Founder of Bloomberg L.P., former Mayor of New York
  • Steve Ballmer — Former CEO of Microsoft
  • Ratan Tata — Industrialist, Tata Group

Law & Judiciary

  • Ruth Bader Ginsburg — U.S. Supreme Court Justice (attended Harvard Law)
  • Elena Kagan — U.S. Supreme Court Justice
  • John Roberts — Chief Justice of the United States
  • Sonia Sotomayor — U.S. Supreme Court Justice

Science, Medicine & Academia

  • Neil deGrasse Tyson — Astrophysicist and science communicator
  • Jennifer Doudna — Co-inventor of CRISPR, Nobel Prize
  • Atul Gawande — Surgeon, writer, public-health leader
  • W.E.B. Du Bois — Sociologist, civil-rights leader (Ph.D.)

Arts, Literature & Entertainment

  • Natalie Portman — Actress (A.B. Psychology)
  • Yo-Yo Ma — Cellist
  • Matt Damon — Actor, screenwriter (attended)
  • Conan O’Brien — Comedian and television host
  • T.S. Eliot — Poet, Nobel laureate
  • Helen Keller — Author and activist

Public Service & International Affairs

  • Henry Kissinger — Former U.S. Secretary of State, Nobel Peace Prize
  • Ban Ki-moon — Former UN Secretary-General
  • Benazir Bhutto — Former Prime Minister of Pakistan
  • Ellen Johnson Sirleaf — Former President of Liberia, Nobel Peace Prize
