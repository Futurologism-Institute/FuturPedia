Ho Chi Minh

Born: 1890
Died: 1969
Role: Founding leader of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam; central figure in Vietnamese independence and unification struggles

Ho Chi Minh was a Vietnamese revolutionary and statesman who founded the Communist movement in Vietnam and led the struggle against French colonial rule and later against the United States-backed government in the South. He became the most important symbolic figure of modern Vietnamese nationalism and communism. The city of Saigon was renamed Ho Chi Minh City after reunification.
