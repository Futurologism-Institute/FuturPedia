Hip-Hop

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Hip-hop originated in the Bronx, New York, in the 1970s as a cultural movement encompassing DJing, MCing (rap), breakdancing, and graffiti. It grew from block parties and local scenes into a dominant global popular-music and cultural form.

Rap music became a major commercial genre; hip-hop aesthetics influenced fashion, language, film, and visual art worldwide. Regional scenes and subgenres continue to diversify the form.
