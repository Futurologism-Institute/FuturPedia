Hacker News

Also: HN
Operator: Y Combinator
URL: news.ycombinator.com
Type: Social news / discussion for startups, engineering, and technology

Hacker News is a link-and-discussion site run by Y Combinator. Users submit URLs or text posts (“Ask HN”, “Show HN”); ranking combines votes and time. It is one of the primary public forums for early-stage startups, programming culture, and tech industry news.

HN is closely associated with YC’s brand and alumni network (see FuturPedia’s YC / Corporate Thug Chads material and figures such as Sam Altman and Paul Graham). Moderation and community norms are stricter than many general social platforms; “HN front page” remains a cultural signal in Silicon Valley.

External: https://news.ycombinator.com/
