Hollywood

Location: Los Angeles, California
Significance: Center of the American film and entertainment industry

Hollywood is the historic and symbolic heart of the U.S. motion-picture industry. Originally a district of Los Angeles, the name has come to represent the broader American film, television, and entertainment business.

Major studios historically associated with Hollywood include Paramount, Warner Bros., MGM, Columbia, Universal, and 20th Century Fox (later Disney). The industry produces films, television series, streaming content, and related media distributed worldwide.
