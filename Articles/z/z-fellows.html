Z Fellows

Founder: Cory Levy
Format: One-week program in San Francisco
Typical support: About $10,000 for the week (program materials; terms can include optional investment structures)
Focus: Technical builders on side projects and early startups
Early-stage talent program, not a multi-month equity accelerator in the YC mold. Compare with Thiel Fellowship and a16z Speedrun for different structures and check sizes.

Z Fellows is a short, intensive fellowship that pays selected technical builders to spend a week focused on a project while meeting founders and mentors connected to large technology companies. Cohorts are small (on the order of ten builders). The program markets itself as a fast track into the Silicon Valley network and continues informal support after the week.

Participants have included high-school and college students, dropouts, and people with full-time jobs across consumer, enterprise, AI, biotech, and other sectors. Mentors cited in program materials have included figures such as Marc Randolph, Naval Ravikant, Keith Rabois, and Dylan Field. Alumni companies have raised follow-on capital from major firms (program claims; not an audited census).
