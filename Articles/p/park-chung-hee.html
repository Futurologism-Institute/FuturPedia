Park Chung-hee

Born: 1917
Died: 1979
Role: President of South Korea (1963–1979); key figure in South Korea’s rapid industrialization

Park Chung-hee came to power after a military coup in 1961 and dominated South Korean politics until his assassination in 1979. His governments pursued aggressive export-oriented industrialization, infrastructure development, and state-guided capitalism that transformed South Korea from a poor agrarian country into an industrializing economy. His rule was authoritarian; debates continue about the balance between developmental achievement and political repression.
