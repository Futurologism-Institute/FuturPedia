History of Poland

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Kingdom & Commonwealth

Poland developed as a medieval kingdom and later formed the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth, one of Europe’s largest states in the early modern period, known for its elective monarchy and relative religious tolerance.

Partitions & Independence

In the late 18th century Poland was partitioned by Russia, Prussia, and Austria and disappeared as a state until 1918. The interwar Second Republic, World War II occupation and devastation, communist rule, Solidarity, and the transition to democracy after 1989 define modern Polish history.
