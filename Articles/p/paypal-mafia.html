PayPal Mafia

Meaning: Informal name for former PayPal employees and founders who later built or funded major Silicon Valley companies
Core era: PayPal (from Confinity / X.com merger) through eBay acquisition (2002) and the following decade of spinouts

The PayPal Mafia is a media and industry label for a cluster of people who worked at PayPal in its formative years and then founded or heavily funded companies including LinkedIn, Palantir, YouTube, Yelp, Tesla (via Elon Musk’s parallel path), SpaceX, and others. Peter Thiel is often described as a central node (“don” in popular accounts); other frequent names include Reid Hoffman, Max Levchin, Elon Musk, David Sacks, Roelof Botha, Keith Rabois, and Jawed Karim / Chad Hurley / Steve Chen (YouTube).

The group’s influence is both operational (founders who had already scaled payments under regulatory and fraud pressure) and financial (early checks and board seats across the 2000s–2010s consumer and enterprise wave).
