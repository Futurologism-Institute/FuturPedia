Politicians of Asia

Selected list of notable Asian politicians and leaders for FuturPedia reference. Not exhaustive. Asia contains the majority of the world’s population and a wide range of political systems.

China

  • Mao Zedong — Founding leader of the People’s Republic of China
  • Deng Xiaoping — Architect of reform and opening-up
  • Xi Jinping — President / General Secretary

India

  • Jawaharlal Nehru — First Prime Minister
  • Indira Gandhi — Prime Minister
  • Manmohan Singh — Prime Minister
  • Narendra Modi — Prime Minister

Japan

  • Shigeru Yoshida — Post-war Prime Minister
  • Shinzo Abe — Prime Minister
  • Fumio Kishida — Prime Minister

Southeast Asia

  • Lee Kuan Yew — Founding Prime Minister of Singapore
  • Mahathir Mohamad — Prime Minister of Malaysia
  • Sukarno & Suharto — Indonesia
  • Ho Chi Minh — Vietnam

Other Asian Leaders

  • Kim Il-sung / Kim Jong-il / Kim Jong-un — North Korea
  • Park Chung-hee & subsequent South Korean leaders
  • Leaders of Pakistan, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Saudi Arabia, Iran, Turkey, and Central Asian states
