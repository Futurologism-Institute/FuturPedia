History of the Philippines

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-colonial & Colonial

Diverse societies inhabited the islands before Spanish colonization beginning in the 16th century. Spanish rule lasted over three centuries, followed by American colonial administration after 1898.

Independence & After

The Philippines became independent in 1946. Postwar politics included democratic periods, the Marcos dictatorship, the 1986 People Power Revolution, and subsequent democratic governments. The country remains a major Southeast Asian nation with strong historical ties to both Spain and the United States.
