History of Portugal

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Formation & Age of Discoveries

Portugal emerged as an independent kingdom in the 12th century. In the 15th–16th centuries it pioneered European maritime exploration, establishing a global trading and colonial empire stretching from Brazil to Africa and Asia.

Modern Period

Later centuries saw decline of imperial dominance, the Braganza monarchy, republican revolution in 1910, the Estado Novo under Salazar, the Carnation Revolution of 1974, decolonization, and integration into the European Union as a democracy.
