Politicians of Africa

Selected list of notable African politicians and leaders for FuturPedia reference. Not exhaustive. Africa’s political landscape is highly diverse across 54 countries.

South Africa

  • Nelson Mandela — President, anti-apartheid leader, Nobel Peace Prize
  • Thabo Mbeki — President
  • Cyril Ramaphosa — President

Nigeria

  • Nnamdi Azikiwe — First President
  • Olusegun Obasanjo — President
  • Muhammadu Buhari — President
  • Bola Tinubu — President

Ghana

  • Kwame Nkrumah — First Prime Minister and President, independence leader
  • John Kufuor — President
  • Nana Akufo-Addo — President

Kenya

  • Jomo Kenyatta — First President
  • Uhuru Kenyatta — President
  • William Ruto — President

Other Notable African Leaders

  • Julius Nyerere — Tanzania (first President, Ujamaa philosophy)
  • Thomas Sankara — Burkina Faso
  • Paul Kagame — Rwanda
  • Ellen Johnson Sirleaf — Liberia (first elected female head of state in Africa, Nobel Peace Prize)
  • Abdel Fattah el-Sisi — Egypt
  • Leaders of Ethiopia, Senegal, Botswana, Mauritius, and other nations

FuturPedia formalization — selected notables.
