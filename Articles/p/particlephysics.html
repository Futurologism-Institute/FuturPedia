Particle Physics — Standard Model

Fermions (matter)
Quarks: up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom (3 colors each)
Leptons: electron, muon, tau + their neutrinos
Three generations of each.
Bosons (force carriers)
• Photon (γ) — electromagnetism
• W⁺, W⁻, Z⁰ — weak interaction
• Gluons (8) — strong interaction
• Higgs boson — gives mass via the Higgs field
Fundamental Interactions
1. Strong (QCD) — holds quarks inside hadrons
2. Electromagnetic (QED)
3. Weak — responsible for beta decay and neutrino interactions
4. Gravity (not yet included in the Standard Model)
Key Features
• Gauge symmetries: SU(3)C × SU(2)L × U(1)Y
• Spontaneous symmetry breaking → Higgs mechanism
• Conservation laws: energy, momentum, charge, baryon number, lepton number (approx.)
Open Questions
Neutrino masses, dark matter, hierarchy problem, quantum gravity, matter-antimatter asymmetry.
