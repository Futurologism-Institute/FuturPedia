Polkadot (DOT)

Polkadot is a multi-chain blockchain platform designed to enable different blockchains to interoperate and share information securely. It was founded by Dr. Gavin Wood (a co-founder of Ethereum) and launched its mainnet in 2020.

Polkadot uses a central “Relay Chain” that provides security and consensus, while independent blockchains called “parachains” connect to it and can specialize in particular use cases. Its native token is DOT, used for governance, staking, and bonding parachains.

The project aims to create a scalable, heterogeneous multi-chain ecosystem rather than a single monolithic blockchain.
