History of Peru (Structural Outline)

Pre-Hispanic
Caral-Supe, Chavín, Moche, Nazca, Wari, Chimú, and Inca Empire (Tawantinsuyu) — the largest pre-Columbian state in the Americas.
Colonial
Spanish conquest (1532 onward), Viceroyalty of Peru, mining economy (Potosí), indigenous resistance and mita labor system.
Independence & Republican
Wars of independence, early republic instability, War of the Pacific, oligarchic republic, APRA and military cycles.
Modern & Contemporary
Military governments, return to democracy, internal conflict (Shining Path era), economic liberalization, recent political volatility and institutional challenges.
