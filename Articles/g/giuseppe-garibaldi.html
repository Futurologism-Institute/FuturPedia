Giuseppe Garibaldi

Born: 1807
Died: 1882
Role: Military leader of the Italian Risorgimento; key figure in Italian unification

Giuseppe Garibaldi was a revolutionary and general whose campaigns, especially the Expedition of the Thousand in 1860, played a decisive role in unifying southern Italy with the emerging Italian kingdom. He became an international symbol of nationalist and liberal struggle.
