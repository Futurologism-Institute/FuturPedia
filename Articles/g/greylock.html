Greylock Partners

Type: Venture capital firm
Founded: 1965
Notable partner (modern era): Reid Hoffman

Greylock Partners is a long-established Silicon Valley venture firm. In the internet and social era it became closely associated with investments and partners including Reid Hoffman (LinkedIn co-founder). The firm backs enterprise and consumer technology companies across stages typical of multi-stage VC.
