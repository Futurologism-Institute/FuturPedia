History of Ghana

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-colonial & Early States

The area of modern Ghana was home to important states including the Akan polities and the powerful Ashanti (Asante) Empire, which rose in the 17th–18th centuries and controlled significant gold and trade networks.

Colonial Period

The British established the Gold Coast colony. Coastal areas came under increasing British control in the 19th century; the Ashanti were defeated in a series of wars, and the colony was consolidated.

Independence and After

Ghana became the first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence from colonial rule on 6 March 1957 under Kwame Nkrumah. Nkrumah pursued pan-Africanism and ambitious development programs before being overthrown in 1966. Subsequent decades saw military and civilian governments, economic challenges, and a return to multiparty democracy in the 1990s. Ghana is often cited as one of West Africa’s more stable democracies in recent decades.
