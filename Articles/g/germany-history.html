History of Germany

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Holy Roman Empire & Early Modern

German-speaking lands were long organized within the Holy Roman Empire, a complex patchwork of states. The Reformation, Thirty Years’ War, and the rise of Prussia and Austria shaped the early modern period.

Unification & Empire

Under Otto von Bismarck, Prussia led the unification of Germany in 1871. The German Empire became a major industrial and military power before World War I.

20th Century & After

Defeat in World War I, the Weimar Republic, the Nazi period and World War II, division into East and West Germany, and reunification in 1990 define modern German history. Germany is now a leading European democracy and economy.
