Jazz

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Jazz originated in the early 20th century in African American communities in the United States, especially New Orleans, drawing on blues, ragtime, spirituals, and brass-band traditions. It emphasizes improvisation, swing rhythm, and individual expression within ensemble playing.

Major styles include early New Orleans jazz, swing, bebop, cool jazz, hard bop, free jazz, fusion, and many later developments. Jazz became a global art form and a major influence on popular music worldwide.
