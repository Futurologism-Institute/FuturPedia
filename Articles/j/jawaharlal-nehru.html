Jawaharlal Nehru

Born: 14 November 1889
Died: 27 May 1964
Role: First Prime Minister of India (1947–1964)

Jawaharlal Nehru was a central leader of the Indian independence movement alongside Mahatma Gandhi and became India’s first prime minister after independence in 1947. He shaped the country’s early democratic institutions, mixed-economy development model, and non-aligned foreign policy.

His tenure covered the integration of princely states, the adoption of the constitution, and the foundational decades of the Republic of India.
