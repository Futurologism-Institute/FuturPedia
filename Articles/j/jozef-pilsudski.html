Józef Piłsudski

Born: 1867
Died: 1935
Role: Chief of State and dominant figure of interwar Poland; key leader in restoring Polish independence

Józef Piłsudski was the central military and political leader in the restoration of Polish independence after World War I. He served as Chief of State, led the country in the Polish–Soviet War, and later returned to power after the 1926 coup, remaining the dominant figure until his death.
