John A. Macdonald

Born: 1815
Died: 1891
Role: First Prime Minister of Canada; key architect of Confederation

John A. Macdonald was the dominant figure in the creation of the Dominion of Canada in 1867 and served as its first prime minister. He promoted national expansion, the transcontinental railway, and a protective economic policy. His legacy is central to Canadian nation-building and also debated in relation to policies toward Indigenous peoples.
