Julius Nyerere

Born: 1922
Died: 1999
Role: First President of Tanzania; major African statesman and theorist of African socialism (Ujamaa)

Julius Nyerere led Tanganyika to independence and became the founding president of Tanzania after the union with Zanzibar. He promoted Ujamaa (African socialism), education, and national unity, and played a significant role in regional and pan-African affairs. He stepped down voluntarily in 1985 and remained an influential elder statesman.
