History of Japan

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Classical & Feudal Periods

Japan developed distinct political and cultural institutions from the classical era (Nara, Heian) through the rise of the samurai and the shogunate systems (Kamakura, Muromachi, Tokugawa). The Tokugawa period (1603–1868) brought long internal peace and relative isolation.

Meiji Restoration & Modernization

The Meiji Restoration (1868) ended the shogunate and launched rapid modernization, industrialization, and the creation of a modern state and empire. Japan became a major power in East Asia.

20th Century & After

Expansion in Asia, involvement in World War II, defeat in 1945, and postwar occupation led to a new constitution and economic recovery. Japan emerged as a major economic power and liberal democracy in the second half of the 20th century and remains a key global actor.
