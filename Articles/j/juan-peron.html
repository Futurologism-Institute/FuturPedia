Juan Domingo Perón

Born: 1895
Died: 1974
Role: President of Argentina; founder of Peronism

Juan Domingo Perón was a dominant figure in 20th-century Argentine politics. As president he built a mass movement combining nationalism, labor rights, and social welfare policies that became known as Peronism. His influence, and that of Eva Perón, continues to shape Argentine political life.
