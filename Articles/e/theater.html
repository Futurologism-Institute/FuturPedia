Theater

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Ancient & Classical

Theater has deep roots in ancient Greek drama, Roman performance, Sanskrit drama, Chinese opera traditions, Japanese Noh and Kabuki, and many other world forms.

Western Development

Medieval religious plays, Renaissance and Shakespearean theater, French classical drama, 19th-century realism, and 20th-century experimental and political theater shaped the Western stage. Broadway, the West End, and national theaters remain major institutions.

Contemporary Theater

Modern theater includes commercial musicals, straight drama, devised work, immersive performance, and global cross-cultural exchange, existing alongside film and digital media rather than being replaced by them.
