Film History

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Origins & Silent Era

Motion pictures emerged in the late 19th century (Edison, Lumière brothers, and others). The silent era established narrative cinema, stars, and major studios, especially in the United States, France, Germany, and the Soviet Union.

Sound, Studio System & Golden Ages

The introduction of synchronized sound in the late 1920s transformed the industry. Hollywood’s studio system dominated for decades. Parallel national cinemas developed distinctive styles (Italian Neorealism, French New Wave, Japanese cinema, Indian popular cinema, and others).

New Hollywood to Digital & Streaming

From the 1960s–70s “New Hollywood” through blockbusters, independent film, global co-productions, and the rise of digital production and streaming platforms, film has continually adapted to technology and audience habits while remaining a central form of mass storytelling.
