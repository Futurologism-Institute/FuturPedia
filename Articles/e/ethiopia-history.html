History of Ethiopia

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Ancient & Medieval Periods

Ethiopia has one of the longest continuous state traditions in Africa. The Kingdom of Aksum was a major Red Sea power. Later Solomonic dynasties claimed continuity with ancient traditions. The Ethiopian Orthodox Church and unique scripts and institutions developed over centuries.

Modern Era

Ethiopia successfully resisted full European colonization (notably defeating Italy at Adwa in 1896). It was occupied by Italy from 1936–1941. Emperor Haile Selassie ruled for much of the 20th century until the 1974 revolution. The Derg military regime followed, then the Ethiopian People’s Revolutionary Democratic Front period, and more recent political changes including the rise of Abiy Ahmed.
