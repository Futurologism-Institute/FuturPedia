History of Egypt

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference. Egyptian history spans millennia.

Ancient Egypt

One of the world’s earliest civilizations, with the unification of Upper and Lower Egypt, the Old, Middle, and New Kingdoms, monumental architecture, hieroglyphic writing, and a long pharaonic tradition.

Greco-Roman, Islamic & Ottoman Periods

Conquest by Alexander, Ptolemaic rule, Roman and Byzantine periods, the Arab-Muslim conquest, successive Islamic dynasties (including the Fatimids and Mamluks), and Ottoman rule shaped the medieval and early modern eras.

Modern Egypt

Muhammad Ali’s modernization in the 19th century, British occupation, independence movements, the 1952 revolution, Nasser’s era, and subsequent presidencies (Sadat, Mubarak, and after) define the modern state. Egypt remains a central political and cultural actor in the Arab world and Africa.
