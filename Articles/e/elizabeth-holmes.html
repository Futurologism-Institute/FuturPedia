Elizabeth Holmes

Born: 3 February 1984
Known for: Founder of Theranos; criminal convictions related to fraud

Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos, a blood-testing company that claimed revolutionary small-sample diagnostics. The firm raised large sums and attracted high-profile board members before investigative reporting and regulatory action exposed that the technology did not perform as marketed. Holmes was convicted on federal fraud charges and sentenced to prison; Theranos dissolved.

The case is a major modern reference point for startup governance, board oversight, and the gap between narrative and clinical reality in health tech.
