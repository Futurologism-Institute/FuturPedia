Animation

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Early Animation

Drawn and stop-motion animation developed in the early 20th century. Studios such as Disney popularized feature-length animated films; other traditions flourished in Europe, the Soviet Union, Japan, and elsewhere.

Television & Global Styles

Television animation became a major form for children and adults. Japanese anime, in particular, developed a global audience and distinctive aesthetic and narrative conventions.

Digital Era

Computer-generated imagery (CGI) transformed both animated features and visual effects in live-action film. Streaming and digital tools have further expanded who can produce and distribute animated work.
