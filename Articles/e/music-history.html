Music — Overview

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Classical & Art Traditions

Western classical music developed through medieval, Renaissance, Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and modern periods. Parallel art-music traditions exist across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and elsewhere.

Popular Music

20th-century popular music — including jazz, blues, rock and roll, pop, hip-hop, electronic music, and countless regional genres — became a dominant global cultural force, driven by recording technology, radio, television, and later digital distribution.

Industry & Technology

The recorded-music industry, live performance, and streaming have continually reshaped how music is made, distributed, and consumed. Music remains both an art form and a major commercial sector.
