Television

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Broadcast Era

Television became a mass medium in the mid-20th century. Network broadcasting in the United States, public broadcasting systems in Europe and elsewhere, and national television industries worldwide shaped news, entertainment, and public culture for decades.

Cable, Satellite & Prestige TV

Cable and satellite expanded channel choice. From the late 1990s onward, long-form serialized drama and comedy elevated television’s artistic status in many markets (“prestige TV”).

Streaming Era

Internet streaming platforms have disrupted traditional schedules and distribution, enabling global simultaneous releases, niche content, and new production models while intensifying competition for attention and rights.
