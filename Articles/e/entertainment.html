Entertainment

Broad category covering film, television, music, theater, and related performing and recorded arts.

Entertainment encompasses the industries and art forms that provide mass and live cultural experiences: cinema, television and streaming, recorded and live music, theater, comedy, and related media. Modern entertainment is global, commercially organized, and deeply intertwined with technology and popular culture.
