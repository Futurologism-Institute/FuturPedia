Video Games

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Origins

Interactive electronic games emerged in the mid-20th century (laboratory experiments, arcade machines, early home consoles). The 1970s and 1980s established arcades, consoles, and personal-computer gaming as commercial industries.

Industry Growth

From the 1990s onward, 3D graphics, online multiplayer, mobile gaming, and digital distribution turned video games into one of the largest entertainment sectors by revenue, with major studios, independent creators, and global esports.

Culture & Technology

Games are simultaneously software, design, narrative, competition, and social platforms. They influence and are influenced by film, music, and online culture, and continue to evolve with hardware, AI, and new platforms.
