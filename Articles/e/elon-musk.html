Elon Musk

Born: 28 June 1971 (Pretoria, South Africa)
Known for: Tesla; SpaceX; xAI; ownership of X (formerly Twitter); Neuralink; The Boring Company

Elon Musk is an entrepreneur and industrialist central to electric vehicles, orbital launch, and large-scale internet platforms. He co-founded Zip2 and X.com (which became part of PayPal), led Tesla as product and manufacturing force in mass-market EVs, and founded SpaceX, which reduced launch costs and pursued reusable rocketry and Starlink broadband.

He acquired Twitter in 2022 and rebranded it X; founded xAI; and has been associated with Neuralink (brain–computer interfaces) and The Boring Company (tunneling). His public role mixes engineering goals, corporate control, and highly visible political and cultural commentary.
