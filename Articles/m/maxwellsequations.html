Maxwell’s Equations

Gauss’s Law for Electricity
∇ · E = ρ / ε₀
Electric field lines begin and end on charges.
Gauss’s Law for Magnetism
∇ · B = 0
There are no magnetic monopoles; field lines form closed loops.
Faraday’s Law
∇ × E = −∂B/∂t
A changing magnetic field induces an electric field.
Ampère–Maxwell Law
∇ × B = μ₀ J + μ₀ ε₀ ∂E/∂t
Currents and changing electric fields produce magnetic fields.
Wave Equation
In free space the equations imply electromagnetic waves traveling at
c = 1 / √(μ₀ ε₀)
