Marc Andreessen

Born: 1971
Known for: Co-author of Mosaic; co-founder of Netscape; co-founder of Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

Marc Andreessen is a software engineer, entrepreneur, and venture capitalist. He co-created the Mosaic web browser, co-founded Netscape, and later co-founded the venture firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), one of Silicon Valley’s most influential investment firms.

a16z’s early-stage accelerator Speedrun operates under the firm’s umbrella. Andreessen is also listed on FuturPedia’s satirical Corporate Thug Chads roster.
