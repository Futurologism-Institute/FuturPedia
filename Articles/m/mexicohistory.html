History of Mexico (Structural Outline)

Pre-Hispanic
Olmec, Maya, Teotihuacan, Toltec, and Aztec (Mexica) civilizations; complex urban societies and tribute empires.
Colonial
Spanish conquest (1519–1521), New Spain viceroyalty, mestizaje, silver economy, Bourbon reforms.
Independence & 19th Century
War of Independence (1810–1821), early republic, Reform War, French Intervention, Porfiriato modernization.
Modern & Contemporary
Mexican Revolution (1910–1920), PRI institutional dominance, democratization, NAFTA/USMCA era, current multiparty system.
