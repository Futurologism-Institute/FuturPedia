Mahatma Gandhi

Born: 1869
Died: 1948
Role: Leader of the Indian independence movement; proponent of nonviolent resistance (satyagraha)

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, known as Mahatma Gandhi, led mass campaigns of nonviolent civil disobedience against British rule in India. His methods and moral leadership influenced independence movements and civil rights struggles worldwide. He was assassinated in 1948, shortly after India achieved independence.
