Mao Zedong

Born: 1893
Died: 1976
Role: Founding leader of the People’s Republic of China; Chairman of the Chinese Communist Party

Mao Zedong led the Chinese Communist Party to victory in the civil war and proclaimed the People’s Republic of China in 1949. He dominated Chinese politics until his death. His rule included land reform, the Great Leap Forward, the Cultural Revolution, and the establishment of a socialist system that later underwent major reform under Deng Xiaoping.
