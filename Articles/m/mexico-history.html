History of Mexico

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-Hispanic & Colonial

Major civilizations including the Olmec, Maya, and Aztec (Mexica) developed in Mesoamerica. Spanish conquest in the early 16th century led to the Viceroyalty of New Spain.

Independence & Modern Mexico

Independence was achieved in the early 19th century. The country experienced foreign interventions, the Reform era, the Porfiriato, the Mexican Revolution (1910–1920), long PRI dominance, and democratization in recent decades. Mexico is a major North American economy and cultural force.
