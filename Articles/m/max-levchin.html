Max Levchin

Born: 1975 (Kyiv; later United States)
Known for: Co-founder and CTO of PayPal; founder/CEO of Affirm; partner at SciFi VC; PayPal Mafia

Max Levchin is a technologist, entrepreneur, and investor. As co-founder and chief technology officer of PayPal, he was central to engineering and fraud systems in the company’s formative years. He later founded Affirm, a consumer finance platform, and serves as its CEO.

With Nellie Levchin he runs SciFi VC, an early-stage fund focused on fintech, AI, and science-heavy startups. He is a core member of the informal PayPal Mafia.
