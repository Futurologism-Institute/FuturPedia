Max Levchin

Born: 1975 (Kyiv; raised partly in the U.S. after immigration)
Known for: Co-founder and CTO of PayPal; founder of Affirm; PayPal Mafia

Max Levchin is a technologist and entrepreneur. As co-founder and chief technology officer of PayPal, he was central to the company’s fraud-fighting and engineering culture in its formative years. After PayPal he founded or co-founded several companies, most prominently Affirm, a consumer finance / buy-now-pay-later platform, where he has served as CEO.

He is a core member of the informal PayPal Mafia and an active investor and board member in technology companies.
