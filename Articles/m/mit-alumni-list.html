MIT — Notable Alumni List

Selected list of notable MIT alumni for FuturPedia reference. Not exhaustive.

Science & Technology

  • Richard Feynman — Nobel Prize in Physics
  • Buzz Aldrin — Apollo 11 astronaut, second person on the Moon
  • Robert Noyce — Co-inventor of the integrated circuit, co-founder of Intel
  • Noam Chomsky — Linguist and public intellectual (faculty emeritus)
  • Ray Kurzweil — Inventor and futurist

Business & Entrepreneurship

  • Drew Houston — Co-founder of Dropbox
  • Kofi Annan — Former UN Secretary-General (Sloan)

Politics & Public Service

  • Ben Bernanke — Former Chair of the Federal Reserve
  • Benjamin Netanyahu — Prime Minister of Israel

Academia

  • Phoebe Wenitte & Wenitte Wenitte — Married couple; Professors of Mathematics and Electrical Engineering
