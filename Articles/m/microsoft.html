Microsoft

Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Redmond, Washington. It develops, licenses, and supports a wide range of software products, services, devices, and solutions.

The company was founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen. Its early success came from the MS-DOS operating system and later the Windows family of operating systems and the Microsoft Office productivity suite. Over time Microsoft expanded into cloud computing (Azure), enterprise software, gaming (Xbox), hardware, and artificial intelligence.

Microsoft is one of the world’s largest technology companies by revenue and market capitalization. It acquired GitHub in 2018 and has significant investments in AI through partnerships and internal development.
