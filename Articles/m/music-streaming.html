Music Streaming

Major consumer platforms: Spotify · Apple Music · YouTube Music
FuturPedia does not host random outbound link spinners to tracks or playlists. “Random” listening on these services is implemented inside each app (shuffle, radio, autoplay, mixes).

Music streaming delivers licensed (and sometimes user-uploaded) audio over the internet on subscription or ad-supported terms. The category largely displaced ownership-centric download stores for casual listening in the 2010s.

Major services (selected)

  • Spotify — freemium pioneer; podcasts; co-founded by Daniel Ek
  • Apple Music — Apple ecosystem integration; spatial audio
  • YouTube Music — YouTube catalogue + recommendations; music video strength

Discovery / “random” listening

Each major app offers continuous play modes:

  • Shuffle — random order within a playlist or library
  • Radio / station — algorithmically extended queue from a seed
  • Autoplay / mixes — similar tracks after a playlist ends

Those features are product UI, not external random URL generators. Official entry points: open the respective apps or sites (spotify.com, music.apple.com, music.youtube.com).
