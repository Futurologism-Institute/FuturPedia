Michael Gibson

Role: Co-founder and General Partner, 1517 Fund
Earlier: Thiel Fellowship / Thiel Foundation leadership; author of Paper Belt on Fire

Michael Gibson is a venture investor and writer. He helped launch and run the Thiel Fellowship with Peter Thiel and later co-founded 1517 Fund with Danielle Strachman. His public writing argues against over-reliance on academic credentials (“the paper belt”) and for taking young technical founders seriously outside standard institutional tracks.
