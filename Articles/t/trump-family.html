Trump Family

American family associated with real estate development in New York and with national politics through Donald J. Trump.

The modern public profile of the Trump family centers on the real-estate business built by Fred Trump and the political career of his son Donald Trump (U.S. President, 2017–2021 and again from 2025). Other branches include professionals outside politics—most notably Donald’s paternal uncle, electrical engineer and MIT professor John G. Trump.

Selected members

  • Frederick Christ Trump (Fred) — New York real-estate developer; father of Donald and brother of John G. Trump
  • Donald J. Trump — businessman; 45th and 47th President of the United States (also listed on Corporate Thug Chads)
  • John G. Trump — MIT electrical engineer; high-voltage research; WWII radar work; cancer radiotherapy applications; National Medal of Science
  • Children and in-laws of Donald Trump active in business and politics (Ivanka, Donald Jr., Eric, Tiffany, Barron, and others) appear frequently in contemporary coverage

Donald Trump has often publicly cited his uncle John’s scientific career when discussing intelligence, nuclear issues, or elite technical institutions.
