History of Turkey

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Ottoman Empire

The Ottoman Empire rose in Anatolia and became a major Islamic and European power for centuries, controlling large territories in the Middle East, North Africa, and Southeast Europe.

Republic of Turkey

After World War I and the collapse of the Ottoman Empire, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk led the War of Independence and founded the Republic of Turkey in 1923. He implemented sweeping secularizing and modernizing reforms. Turkey has since oscillated between periods of military influence and civilian democratic politics and remains a key regional power.
