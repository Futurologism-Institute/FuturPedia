History of Tanzania

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Colonial Period

Mainland Tanzania (Tanganyika) was under German rule and later became a British mandate after World War I. Zanzibar was a separate sultanate under British influence.

Independence & Union

Tanganyika gained independence in 1961 under Julius Nyerere. Zanzibar became independent in 1963; the two united in 1964 to form the United Republic of Tanzania. Nyerere pursued African socialism (Ujamaa) and became a major figure in African politics and the Non-Aligned Movement.
