Tesla, Inc.

Tesla, Inc. is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company. It designs, manufactures, and sells electric vehicles, battery energy storage systems, solar products, and related software and services.

The company was incorporated in 2003 as Tesla Motors by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. Elon Musk joined shortly afterward, became the largest shareholder, and later assumed the roles of CEO and product architect. Tesla’s first vehicle was the Roadster; subsequent models include the Model S, Model X, Model 3, Model Y, Cybertruck, and others.

Tesla is known for advancing electric vehicle adoption, battery technology, autonomous driving software (Autopilot / Full Self-Driving), and energy products such as the Powerwall and Megapack. It operates factories in the United States, China, Germany, and other locations.
