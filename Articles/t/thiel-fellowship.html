Thiel Fellowship

Founded: 2011 by Peter Thiel (via the Thiel Foundation)
Grant: $250,000 over two years (as of recent classes; earlier cohorts received $100,000)
Eligibility: Generally age 22 or younger and without a completed university degree; fellows must leave formal university enrollment to accept

The Thiel Fellowship is a program that provides young people with funding and network access to pursue ambitious projects — typically startups, scientific research, or other ventures — instead of following a conventional college path. It takes no equity in the fellows’ companies.

Fellows gain mentorship and connections within the Thiel Foundation’s network of founders, investors, and scientists. The program has supported several hundred fellows since its launch and is associated with companies and projects of significant collective value.
