Thiel Fellowship — Notable Alumni List

Selected list of notable Thiel Fellowship alumni for FuturPedia reference. The program has supported several hundred fellows since 2011; this is not an exhaustive roster.

Technology & Software

  • Vitalik Buterin (2014) — Co-founder of Ethereum
  • Dylan Field (2012) — Co-founder & CEO of Figma
  • Chris Olah — Co-founder of Anthropic

Hardware, Autonomy & Deep Tech

  • Austin Russell (2013) — Founder & CEO of Luminar Technologies
  • Laura Deming (2011) — Founder of The Longevity Fund / age1

Consumer & Other Ventures

  • Ritesh Agarwal (2013) — Founder & CEO of OYO Rooms
  • Boyan Slat (2016) — Founder of The Ocean Cleanup
  • Dale Stephens (2011) — Founder of UnCollege / Year On

Additional Notes

Other fellows have founded or contributed to companies and projects across AI, biotech, space, logistics, fintech, and more. Collective valuations of companies associated with alumni have reached into the hundreds of billions of dollars according to various reports.
