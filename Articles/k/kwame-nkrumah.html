Kwame Nkrumah

Born: 1909
Died: 1972
Role: First Prime Minister and first President of Ghana; leading pan-Africanist

Kwame Nkrumah led the Gold Coast to independence in 1957, making Ghana the first sub-Saharan African colony to achieve independence. He became a central figure in pan-Africanism, advocating African unity and non-alignment.

His government pursued ambitious industrialization and educational expansion but faced economic difficulties and growing authoritarianism. He was overthrown in a 1966 coup while abroad. Nkrumah remains a major symbolic figure in African independence history.
