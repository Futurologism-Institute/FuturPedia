History of Korea

Selected overview covering the peninsula for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-modern Korea

Successive kingdoms (including Gojoseon, the Three Kingdoms, Goryeo, and Joseon) developed a distinct civilization with Confucian institutions, a unique writing system (Hangul), and complex relations with China and Japan.

Colonial Period & Division

Japan annexed Korea in 1910. After Japan’s defeat in 1945 the peninsula was divided. The Korean War (1950–1953) solidified the division into North and South.

North and South

South Korea experienced authoritarian developmental states, rapid industrialization, democratization from the late 1980s, and emergence as a major economy and cultural exporter. North Korea developed a hereditary socialist system under the Kim family with a strong emphasis on military strength and isolation.
