History of Kenya

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-colonial & Early Contact

Kenya’s highlands and coastal regions were inhabited by diverse Bantu, Nilotic, and Cushitic-speaking peoples. The coast had long-standing trade links with the Indian Ocean world (Swahili city-states).

Colonial Period

British control expanded in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The East Africa Protectorate became the Colony of Kenya. White settlement in the highlands and African resistance (including the Mau Mau uprising in the 1950s) shaped the late colonial period.

Independence and After

Kenya gained independence on 12 December 1963 under Jomo Kenyatta. The country experienced one-party dominance for many years, multiparty reforms in the 1990s, and periods of political tension (including post-election violence in 2007–08). It remains one of East Africa’s major economies and political actors.
