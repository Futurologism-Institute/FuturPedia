Kian Sadeghi

Known for: Founder of Nucleus (consumer genomics / genetic insights)
Programs: Z Fellows alum; Thiel Fellowship support (publicly noted alongside Founders Fund backing)

Kian Sadeghi is a founder associated with Nucleus, a company in consumer genomics and genetic analysis. He has been publicly linked to early support from Cory Levy / the Z Fellows network and later to the Thiel Fellowship and Founders Fund–adjacent capital, illustrating the overlap between short Silicon Valley talent programs and longer fellowship tracks.
