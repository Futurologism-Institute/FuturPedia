Kian Sadeghi

Role: Founder and CEO, Nucleus (consumer genomics)
Education: Studied computational biology at the University of Pennsylvania; left without completing a conventional degree path
Programs: Z Fellows; Thiel Fellowship; early support from Cory Levy and Founders Fund–adjacent capital

Kian Sadeghi is the founder of Nucleus (also referred to as Nucleus Genomics), a company focused on whole-genome sequencing and consumer genetic analysis for health and related insights. He has described personal and scientific motivations for making genome-scale information more accessible, and the company has drawn both substantial venture interest and public controversy around trait prediction and marketing claims.

Sadeghi attended the University of Pennsylvania (computational biology) before leaving to build Nucleus. He has publicly credited early support from Cory Levy and the Z Fellows network, later Thiel Fellowship recognition, and investment connected to Founders Fund and other Silicon Valley backers (including rounds reported with participation from Alexis Ohanian’s 776 and others).
