XRP

XRP is a digital asset designed for fast, low-cost cross-border payments. It was created by Ripple Labs (originally OpenCoin) and launched in 2012.

Unlike many cryptocurrencies that rely on mining, XRP uses a consensus protocol among trusted validators. Transactions on the XRP Ledger typically settle in seconds with very low fees. Ripple promotes XRP as a bridge currency for international money transfers between financial institutions.

XRP has been the subject of regulatory attention, particularly in the United States. It remains one of the largest cryptocurrencies by market capitalization and is widely used in payment-related applications.
