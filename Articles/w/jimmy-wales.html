Jimmy Wales

Born: 7 August 1966
Role: Co-founder of Wikipedia; founder of the Wikimedia Foundation (early leadership)

Jimmy Wales is an internet entrepreneur best known as co-founder of Wikipedia. With Larry Sanger, he launched Wikipedia in January 2001 after the earlier Nupedia project. Wales became the public face of the project and remained a central figure in its governance culture and in the broader free-knowledge movement.

He has also been associated with other wiki and free-culture initiatives and remains one of the most recognizable figures in collaborative online knowledge production.
