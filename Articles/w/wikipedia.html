Wikipedia

Launched: 15 January 2001
Type: Free, collaboratively edited online encyclopedia
Operator: Wikimedia Foundation
Co-founders: Jimmy Wales, Larry Sanger

Wikipedia is a multilingual, free online encyclopedia written and maintained by volunteer editors. It became one of the most widely used reference sites on the internet and a default starting point for general-knowledge lookup.

It grew out of an earlier project, Nupedia, and adopted a wiki model that allowed rapid, open editing. Content policies emphasize verifiability, neutral point of view, and no original research. FuturPedia lists Wikipedia first among acceptable sources for straight encyclopedia-style drafting.
