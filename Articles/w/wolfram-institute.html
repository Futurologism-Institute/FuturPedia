The Wolfram Institute for Computational Foundations of Science studies simple computational rules underlying complex scientific phenomena—and how the universe and science itself can be understood as emerging from them. It is a nonprofit research initiative in the orbit of Stephen Wolfram’s long-running work on cellular automata, the Wolfram Physics Project, the ruliad, and multicomputation.

Research themes include fundamental physics (hypergraphs, discrete gravity, quantum foundations), mathematical foundations, biology and complex systems, machine learning / minds, and philosophy of computation. Output includes computational essays and open research notes; the Institute wiki is the official collaborative documentation layer.
