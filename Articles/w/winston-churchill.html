Winston Churchill

Born: 1874
Died: 1965
Role: Prime Minister of the United Kingdom (1940–1945, 1951–1955); central figure in the Allied victory in World War II

Winston Churchill led Britain during the critical years of World War II. His wartime leadership, speeches, and strategic decisions made him one of the most prominent statesmen of the 20th century. He also had a long earlier career and a second premiership in the 1950s. He was awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature.
