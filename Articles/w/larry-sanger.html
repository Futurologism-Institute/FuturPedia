Larry Sanger

Born: 16 July 1968
Role: Co-founder of Wikipedia; early organizer of Nupedia and Wikipedia editorial process

Larry Sanger is a philosopher and internet project organizer best known as co-founder of Wikipedia alongside Jimmy Wales. He served as editor-in-chief of Nupedia and played a central role in proposing and structuring Wikipedia’s early wiki-based model and community processes.

Sanger left the project in its early years and later became a public critic of aspects of Wikipedia’s culture and governance, while remaining historically recognized as a co-founder of the encyclopedia.
