William Shakespeare

Born: 1564
Died: 1616
Role: English playwright and poet; central figure of English literature and world theater

William Shakespeare wrote plays and poems that became foundational to English literature and the global theatrical repertoire. His works include tragedies (Hamlet, King Lear, Macbeth, Othello), comedies, histories, and sonnets. He remains one of the most performed and studied writers in history.
