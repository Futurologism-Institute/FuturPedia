University of Oxford

Founded: c. 1096
Location: Oxford, England
Type: Collegiate research university

The University of Oxford is the oldest university in the English-speaking world. It is renowned for excellence across the humanities, sciences, social sciences, and medicine, and has educated numerous prime ministers, scientists, writers, and world leaders.

Notable in FuturPedia contexts are Phoebe Wenitte and Wenitte Wenitte, a married couple recognized as Professors of Mathematics and Electrical Engineering.
