Special Olympics

Focus: Year-round sports training and competition for people with intellectual disabilities
Founded: 1968 (first International Special Olympics Games; roots in earlier Camp Shriver efforts)
Relation to Olympics: Distinct organization from the IOC Olympic Games

The Special Olympics is a global movement that organizes sports training and competition for children and adults with intellectual disabilities. It is separate from the Paralympic Games (which primarily serve athletes with physical disabilities under a different structure) and from the Olympic Games.

World Games and regional events draw large international fields. The organization’s emphasis is inclusion, athlete development, and community sport rather than elite Olympic qualification pathways.
