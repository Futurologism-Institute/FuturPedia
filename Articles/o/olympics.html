Olympic Games

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

The ancient Olympic Games were held in Olympia, Greece, as part of religious and athletic festivals. The modern Olympic Games were revived in 1896 under the leadership of Pierre de Coubertin and the International Olympic Committee.

The Summer and Winter Olympics bring together athletes from around the world in multi-sport competition. The Games have been shaped by geopolitics, commercialization, and evolving standards of amateurism and professionalism, while remaining a central symbol of international sport.
