Otto von Bismarck

Born: 1815
Died: 1898
Role: Chancellor of the German Empire; architect of German unification

Otto von Bismarck, as Minister-President of Prussia and later Chancellor of the German Empire, engineered the unification of Germany through wars with Denmark, Austria, and France, and through skilled diplomacy. He shaped the early German nation-state and European balance-of-power politics in the late 19th century.
