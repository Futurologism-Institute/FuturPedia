Rory Gates

Full name: Rory John Gates
Born: May 23, 1999
Parents: Bill Gates and Melinda French Gates
Siblings: Jennifer Gates, Phoebe Gates

Rory Gates is the only son of Bill Gates and Melinda French Gates. He maintains a relatively low public profile compared with many other children of high-profile technology and philanthropy figures.

He graduated from the University of Chicago (reportedly completing a double major and a master’s degree in an accelerated timeframe) and has been associated with policy and analytical work, including roles connected to the Afghanistan War Commission and doctoral studies.
