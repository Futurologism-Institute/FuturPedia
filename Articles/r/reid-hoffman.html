Reid Hoffman

Born: 4 August 1967
Known for: Co-founder of LinkedIn; partner at Greylock Partners; early PayPal executive (PayPal Mafia)

Reid Hoffman is an entrepreneur and venture capitalist. He was an early executive at PayPal and co-founded LinkedIn, which became the dominant professional social network and was later acquired by Microsoft. He is a partner at Greylock Partners and a prolific angel and board investor across consumer and enterprise technology.

Hoffman is also a public writer and podcast host on entrepreneurship, networks, and AI. He is routinely counted among the core PayPal Mafia.
