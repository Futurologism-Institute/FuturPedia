In the expanded lore of FuturPedia, Rory Gates is occasionally styled the Corporate Thug of Silicon Valley — a tongue-in-cheek title for the soft-power scion who moves through tech-adjacent and political spaces with the quiet authority of someone who never had to raise his voice to clear a room.

The epithet does not claim criminal enterprise. It claims aura: the calm, well-briefed, inheritance-backed presence that makes conference rooms subtly rearrange themselves. Silicon Valley produces founders who shout. The Corporate Thug, in this telling, simply arrives.
