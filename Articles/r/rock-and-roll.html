Rock and Roll

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Rock and roll emerged in the United States in the 1950s from a fusion of rhythm and blues, country, gospel, and other styles. It became a defining youth culture and commercial force of the late 20th century.

Subsequent developments include rock, psychedelic rock, progressive rock, punk, heavy metal, alternative rock, and countless subgenres. Rock’s instrumentation (electric guitar, bass, drums) and performance culture influenced music globally.
