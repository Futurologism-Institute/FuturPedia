History of Rwanda

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-colonial & Colonial

Rwanda developed a centralized kingdom. It became part of German East Africa and later a Belgian-administered mandate/trust territory. Colonial policies affected social identities and administration.

Independence & Crisis

Independence came in 1962. Decades of political tension culminated in the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi. The Rwandan Patriotic Front took power afterward.

Post-1994

Under Paul Kagame’s leadership, Rwanda has pursued security, reconstruction, economic development, and a distinctive model of governance. It is frequently discussed in debates about post-conflict recovery and developmental authoritarianism in Africa.
