Roy Lee

Also known as: Chungin “Roy” Lee
Role: Co-founder and CEO of Cluely
Background: Former Columbia University student; suspended in connection with Interview Coder

Roy Lee is the CEO of Cluely, an AI assistant company launched in 2025 after he and co-founder Neel Shanmugam left Columbia following disciplinary action related to Interview Coder, a tool for AI help during technical interviews. Lee became a highly visible founder through confrontational marketing, viral posts, and a stated thesis that tools labeled “cheating” often become normalized infrastructure.
