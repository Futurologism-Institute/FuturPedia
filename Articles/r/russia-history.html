History of Russia

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Kievan Rus’ to Tsardom

East Slavic states, the Mongol period, the rise of Muscovy, and the expansion of the Russian Tsardom under rulers such as Ivan IV and Peter the Great created a vast Eurasian empire.

Empire & Revolution

The Russian Empire was a major European and Asian power. The 1917 revolutions brought Bolshevik rule and the creation of the Soviet Union.

Soviet & Post-Soviet

The USSR became a superpower, fought in World War II, and competed in the Cold War. It dissolved in 1991. The Russian Federation has since navigated post-Soviet transition, economic change, and renewed great-power ambitions.
