History of India

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference. India’s history spans millennia.

Ancient & Classical Periods

The Indus Valley Civilization, Vedic period, rise of Buddhism and Jainism, Mauryan Empire (including Ashoka), Gupta period, and successive regional kingdoms shaped classical Indian civilization.

Medieval Period

Islamic sultanates, the Delhi Sultanate, and the Mughal Empire (from the 16th century) created major political and cultural formations across large parts of the subcontinent.

Colonial Period & Independence

British power expanded from the 18th century through the East India Company and later direct Crown rule. The independence movement, led by figures including Mahatma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Nehru, and many others, culminated in independence and partition in 1947. The Republic of India was established in 1950.

Republic of India

Post-independence India has been a federal parliamentary democracy. Major themes include economic development, non-alignment (earlier decades), liberalization from the 1990s, and its emergence as a major global power.
