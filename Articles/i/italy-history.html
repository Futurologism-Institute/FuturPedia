History of Italy

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Ancient & Medieval

The Roman Republic and Empire, the fall of the Western Empire, and the rise of city-states, the Papacy, and regional powers shaped the Italian peninsula for centuries.

Unification

The Risorgimento in the 19th century, led by figures such as Giuseppe Garibaldi and Camillo Cavour, unified most of Italy under the House of Savoy by 1870–71.

20th Century & After

Liberal Italy, Fascism under Mussolini, World War II, the postwar republic, economic transformation, and membership in European institutions define modern Italian history.
