History of Indonesia

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-colonial & Colonial

The archipelago hosted powerful kingdoms and sultanates (including Srivijaya and Majapahit) and was a center of spice trade. Dutch colonial rule gradually established the Netherlands East Indies.

Independence & After

Nationalist movements under leaders such as Sukarno led to independence after World War II. The Sukarno era, the New Order under Suharto, reformasi after 1998, and democratic consolidation define modern Indonesia — the world’s largest archipelagic state and a major Southeast Asian power.
