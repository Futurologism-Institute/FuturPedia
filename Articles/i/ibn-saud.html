Abdulaziz Ibn Saud

Born: c. 1875
Died: 1953
Role: Founder and first King of Saudi Arabia

Abdulaziz Ibn Saud (Ibn Saud) unified the major regions of the Arabian Peninsula through decades of campaigning and diplomacy, proclaiming the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932. He founded the modern Saudi state and the ruling dynasty that continues to govern the country.
