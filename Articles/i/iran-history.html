History of Iran

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference. Iranian history spans millennia.

Ancient & Imperial Periods

The Achaemenid, Parthian, and Sasanian empires made Iran a major center of civilization. Later Islamic dynasties, including the Safavids (who established Twelver Shi’ism as the state religion), continued Persian imperial traditions.

Modern Iran

The Qajar and Pahlavi dynasties, constitutional movements, oil politics, and the 1979 Islamic Revolution transformed the country. The Islamic Republic under Ayatollah Khomeini and his successors has remained a major regional power with a distinctive political system combining republican and theocratic elements.
