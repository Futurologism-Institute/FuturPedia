Daniel Ek

Born: 21 February 1983 (Stockholm, Sweden)
Role: Co-founder and CEO of Spotify
Also: Investor and chairman roles in related European tech

Daniel Ek is a Swedish entrepreneur best known as co-founder and chief executive of Spotify, the audio streaming platform that became one of the dominant global services for music and podcasts. With co-founder Martin Lorentzon, he launched Spotify in the late 2000s, combining licensed catalogues with freemium access and later expanding into podcasts and other audio.

Under Ek’s leadership Spotify listed publicly and competed with Apple, Amazon, YouTube, and others in the streaming market. He has been a prominent face of the European tech scale-up story and an active investor in other companies.
