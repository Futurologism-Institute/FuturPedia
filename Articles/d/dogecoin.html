Dogecoin (DOGE)

Dogecoin is a cryptocurrency that began as a lighthearted meme-based alternative to Bitcoin. It was created in 2013 by software engineers Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer, featuring the Shiba Inu dog from the “Doge” internet meme.

Dogecoin uses a proof-of-work consensus mechanism similar to Litecoin (scrypt-based) and has no fixed supply cap; new coins continue to be mined. It gained widespread popularity through internet culture, social media, and high-profile endorsements, particularly in 2021.

Despite its origins as a joke, Dogecoin has maintained a large community and significant market presence, and is used for tipping, payments, and as a speculative asset.
