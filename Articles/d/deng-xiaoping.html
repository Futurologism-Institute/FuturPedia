Deng Xiaoping

Born: 22 August 1904
Died: 19 February 1997
Role: Paramount leader of China (late 1970s–1990s); architect of “Reform and Opening-up”

Deng Xiaoping emerged as China’s paramount leader after the death of Mao Zedong. He shifted national policy toward economic reform, market mechanisms, foreign investment, and gradual opening to the global economy while maintaining Communist Party political control.

These policies are widely credited with launching China’s decades of rapid economic growth and transformation into a major global power.
