Lamar Jackson

Born: 7 January 1997
Sport: American football — quarterback, Baltimore Ravens (NFL)
Notable: Multiple-time NFL MVP; dual-threat quarterback

Lamar Jackson is the starting quarterback of the Baltimore Ravens. He is known for combining elite rushing ability with passing production and has won multiple league MVP awards. He is among the most visible modern NFL players.

Public interactions with national politics have included social-media exchanges and later presidential remarks referring to him and his family in connection with Ravens team appearances at events—separate from his on-field role.
