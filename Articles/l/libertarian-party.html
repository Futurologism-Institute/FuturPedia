Libertarian Party (United States)

The Libertarian Party is a political party in the United States that advocates for classical liberal and libertarian principles. It was founded in 1971 in Colorado Springs, Colorado.

The party’s core positions emphasize individual liberty, free markets, limited government, non-interventionist foreign policy, and the protection of civil liberties. It generally supports reducing the size and scope of government, ending victimless-crime laws, and promoting personal responsibility.

Although it is the largest third party in the United States by voter registration in many states, it has not won major federal offices. The party nominates candidates for president and other offices and maintains a national committee and state affiliates.
