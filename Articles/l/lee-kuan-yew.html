Lee Kuan Yew

Born: 16 September 1923
Died: 23 March 2015
Role: Founding Prime Minister of Singapore (1959–1990)

Lee Kuan Yew led Singapore from self-government through independence (1965) and into rapid economic transformation. Under his leadership and that of the People’s Action Party, Singapore moved from a developing port city to a global financial and trading hub.

His governance model emphasized pragmatism, anti-corruption, long-term planning, and strict social discipline. He remains one of the most studied figures in development and state-building.
