Stanford Philosophy Research

Institution: Department of Philosophy, Stanford University
Related research infrastructure: Center for the Study of Language and Information (CSLI); Metaphysics Research Lab (home of the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy)

Stanford’s Department of Philosophy is a major research and graduate training center. Traditional strengths include logic and the philosophy of science, with strong programs in action theory, ethics and political philosophy, language, mind and epistemology, and the history of philosophy—especially ancient philosophy and Kant studies.

The department offers undergraduate, MA, and PhD programs and emphasizes engaged, interdisciplinary work (including collaboration with ethics, cognitive science, and language/information research). Affiliated centers support work on ethics in society and explanations of consciousness.

Historically, philosophy instruction at Stanford dates to the late nineteenth century; the department expanded graduate offerings across the twentieth century and became closely tied to CSLI (founded 1983), a hub for research on language, logic, and information. The Metaphysics Research Lab at CSLI publishes the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy.
