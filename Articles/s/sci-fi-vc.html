Sci Fi VC

Sense used here: Informal label for venture capital aimed at science-fiction-adjacent, deep-tech, or “future of everything” theses—not a single canonical firm name in all contexts
“Sci Fi VC” is used in industry slang more than as one exclusive brand. Related formal programs and funds in FuturPedia’s orbit include 1517 Fund (dropouts / renegade science), parts of Founders Fund’s portfolio rhetoric, and other deep-tech specialists. If you meant a specific fund named Sci Fi VC, clarify and the page can be retargeted.

In practice the phrase points at investors who deliberately fund hardware, space, longevity, advanced compute, and other themes that sound like science fiction until they ship. It contrasts with pure consumer-social or pure SaaS pattern-matching.
