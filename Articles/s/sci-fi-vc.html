SciFi VC

Also styled: Sci Fi VC
Founded: 2017 (roots in HVF / Levchin investment activity)
Principals: Max Levchin and Nellie Levchin
HQ: San Francisco
Site: scifi.vc

SciFi VC is the early-stage venture firm of Max and Nellie Levchin. It invests as a generalist with emphasis on fintech, artificial intelligence, and science (energy, biotech, BCI, quantum, and related deep tech), plus other hard technical or regulated categories.

The fund is operator-led: Max Levchin’s background as PayPal co-founder/CTO and Affirm CEO shapes a thesis around difficult technical problems, network effects, and complex industries. SciFi grew out of earlier Levchin investment activity (including HVF).
