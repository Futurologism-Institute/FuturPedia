Solana (SOL)

Solana is a high-performance blockchain platform designed for scalable decentralized applications and crypto assets. It was founded by Anatoly Yakovenko and others and launched in 2020.

Solana aims to achieve high transaction throughput and low fees through a combination of proof-of-stake consensus and a novel mechanism called proof-of-history, which provides a cryptographic clock for ordering transactions.

Its native token is SOL. Solana has become a major platform for decentralized finance (DeFi), NFTs, and consumer-facing crypto applications, known for its speed relative to earlier smart-contract platforms.
