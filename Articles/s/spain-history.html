History of Spain

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Medieval & Imperial

Visigothic rule, the Muslim conquest and Al-Andalus, the Christian Reconquista, and the unification of Castile and Aragon created the basis of modern Spain. Spain built a vast global empire in the Americas and elsewhere from the late 15th century.

Modern Period

Decline of imperial power, the Napoleonic invasion, 19th-century instability, the Spanish Civil War (1936–1939), the Franco dictatorship, and the transition to democracy after 1975 define modern Spanish history. Spain is now a constitutional monarchy and EU member.
