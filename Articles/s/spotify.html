Spotify

Founded: 2006 (Sweden); public service launch 2008
Co-founders: Daniel Ek, Martin Lorentzon
Type: Audio streaming (music, podcasts, and related)

Spotify is a major global audio streaming platform. It popularized large-scale freemium access to licensed music catalogues and later became a significant distributor of podcasts. The company is publicly traded and competes with technology and media firms across subscription and ad-supported tiers.
