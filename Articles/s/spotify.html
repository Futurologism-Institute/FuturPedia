Spotify

Founded: 2006 (Sweden); public service launch 2008
Co-founders: Daniel Ek, Martin Lorentzon
Type: Audio streaming (music, podcasts, and related)
Official site: spotify.com

Spotify is a major global audio streaming platform. It popularized large-scale freemium access to licensed music catalogues and later became a significant distributor of podcasts. The company is publicly traded and competes with Apple Music, YouTube Music, and others.

In-app discovery includes shuffle, radio/stations, Daily Mix-style playlists, and autoplay—continuous listening controlled by the service, not random external link dumps.
