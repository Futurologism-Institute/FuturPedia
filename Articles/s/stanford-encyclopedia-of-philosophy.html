Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy

Also known as: SEP; “Stanford Plato” (from the domain plato.stanford.edu)
Launched: 1995
Founder / principal editor (founding): Edward N. Zalta
Publisher: Metaphysics Research Lab, Center for the Study of Language and Information (CSLI), Stanford University
URL: plato.stanford.edu

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (SEP) is a freely available, peer-reviewed online encyclopedia of philosophy. Each entry is written and maintained by a specialist author, refereed by an editorial board, and updated over time so the reference work does not freeze the way a print encyclopedia does.

The site’s hostname—plato.stanford.edu—leads many people to call it “Stanford Plato.” Entries cover historical figures (including a major entry on Plato), contemporary debates, and technical subfields. Authors retain copyright while granting Stanford permission to publish. The project began with U.S. public funding and is sustained in part by university library support for long-term open access.
