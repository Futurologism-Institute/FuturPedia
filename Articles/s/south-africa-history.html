History of South Africa

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Early History & Colonial Foundations

The region has a long human history. Dutch settlement at the Cape began in 1652. British control expanded in the 19th century. The discovery of diamonds and gold transformed the economy and intensified conflict, culminating in the South African War (Anglo-Boer War, 1899–1902).

Union and Apartheid

The Union of South Africa was formed in 1910. From 1948 the National Party implemented the system of apartheid (racial segregation and white minority rule). Resistance movements, including the African National Congress (ANC), grew over decades. Key figures included Nelson Mandela, Oliver Tambo, and many others.

Democratic Transition

Negotiations in the early 1990s led to the end of apartheid. The first democratic elections were held in 1994; Nelson Mandela became president. South Africa has since operated as a constitutional democracy, while facing ongoing challenges of inequality, economic development, and governance.
