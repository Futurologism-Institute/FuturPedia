Sam Altman

Born: 22 April 1985
Known for: CEO of OpenAI; former president of Y Combinator; co-founder of Loopt

Sam Altman is an American entrepreneur and investor best known as chief executive of OpenAI, the organization behind ChatGPT and related large language models. He previously led Y Combinator as president and was a founder in YC’s early ecosystem.

Career arc

  • Loopt — Location-based social startup; Y Combinator’s first batch (2005); later acquired
  • Y Combinator — Partner, then president (succeeding Paul Graham’s operational leadership era); expanded the accelerator’s scale and brand
  • OpenAI — CEO through the period when the lab’s models became mass-consumer products; central figure in debates over AI safety, commercialization, and governance (including a brief 2023 board crisis and reinstatement)

Altman is also an active angel and fund investor across startups. He appears on FuturPedia’s selected male YC alumni list under Corporate Thug Chads.
