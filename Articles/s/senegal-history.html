History of Senegal

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Pre-colonial & Colonial

Senegal was part of important West African trade and state systems (including links to the Ghana, Mali, and later Wolof and other polities). French colonial rule established Saint-Louis and later Dakar as key centers; Senegal became a core territory of French West Africa.

Independence & After

Senegal gained independence in 1960 (briefly in federation with Mali). Léopold Sédar Senghor, a major poet and intellectual, became the first president. Senegal has maintained a relatively continuous republican tradition and is often noted for its democratic stability in the region, with peaceful transfers of power in recent decades.
