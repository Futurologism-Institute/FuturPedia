History of Saudi Arabia

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Early & Unification

The Arabian Peninsula was home to early Islamic history and successive tribal and regional powers. In the 18th–20th centuries the Al Saud family, in alliance with Wahhabi religious reformers, expanded control. Abdulaziz Ibn Saud unified most of the peninsula and proclaimed the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932.

Modern Kingdom

The discovery of oil transformed the economy and global role of Saudi Arabia. The kingdom has remained an absolute monarchy under the Al Saud, a central player in energy markets, Islam’s holy cities, and Middle Eastern politics.
