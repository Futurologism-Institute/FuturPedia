History of Singapore

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Early & Colonial Period

Singapore has long been a trading point in the Malay world. Modern Singapore was founded as a British trading post by Stamford Raffles in 1819 and grew into a major port within the Straits Settlements.

20th Century

The city experienced Japanese occupation during World War II. After the war it moved toward self-government. Singapore joined the Federation of Malaysia in 1963 and became an independent republic in 1965 after separation.

Independent Singapore

Under the leadership of Lee Kuan Yew and the People’s Action Party, Singapore pursued rapid economic development, strict governance, and transformation into a global financial and trading hub. It is widely studied as a case of successful post-colonial development.
