Sukarno

Born: 1901
Died: 1970
Role: First President of Indonesia; leader of the independence movement

Sukarno led Indonesia’s struggle for independence from the Netherlands and became the country’s first president. He promoted nationalism, guided democracy, and non-alignment, and was a prominent figure in the early Non-Aligned Movement. He was sidelined after the mid-1960s political crisis that brought Suharto to power.
