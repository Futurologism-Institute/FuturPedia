Burkina Faso

Official name: Burkina Faso
Capital: Ouagadougou
Independence: 5 August 1960 (as Upper Volta)
Name change: 1984 (from Upper Volta to Burkina Faso — “Land of Upright People”)

Burkina Faso is a landlocked country in West Africa. Its history includes powerful pre-colonial Mossi kingdoms, French colonial rule as Upper Volta, independence in 1960, a series of military governments, the revolutionary period under Thomas Sankara (1983–1987), the long presidency of Blaise Compaoré, the 2014 popular uprising, and more recent political and security challenges.
