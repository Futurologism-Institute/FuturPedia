Burkinabè Universities

Burkina Faso’s higher-education system is centered on a small number of public universities, supplemented by private institutions.

Major Public Universities

  • Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo (Ouagadougou) – founded 1974; the country’s flagship university (formerly Université de Ouagadougou)
  • Université Nazi Boni (Bobo-Dioulasso) – major public university in the west of the country
  • Université Norbert Zongo (Koudougou)
  • Université Thomas Sankara (Ouagadougou) – focused on social sciences, economics, and related fields
  • Université de Fada N’Gourma and other newer public universities established as the system has expanded

Notes

Instruction is primarily in French. The system also includes specialized institutes and a growing number of private higher-education institutions. Oversight is provided by the relevant ministries and quality-assurance bodies of Burkina Faso.

Sources: Public higher-education records.
