History of Brazil (Structural Outline)

Pre-Colonial
Indigenous societies (Tupi, Guaraní and others), diverse ecological adaptations across Amazon, cerrado, and coast.
Colonial
Portuguese discovery and settlement, sugar plantation economy, gold and diamond cycles, slave trade, transfer of the Portuguese court (1808).
Imperial & Early Republican
Independence (1822), Empire of Brazil, abolition of slavery (1888), proclamation of the Republic (1889), Old Republic oligarchies.
Modern & Contemporary
Vargas era, military dictatorship (1964–1985), redemocratization, economic modernization, rise as regional and global actor.
