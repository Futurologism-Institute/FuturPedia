Brendan Foody

Role: Co-founder and CEO, Mercor
Programs: Thiel Fellow (2024 class with co-founders)
Education: Georgetown University (left without completing conventional path)

Brendan Foody is co-founder and CEO of Mercor, a company that matches skilled human experts with AI labs and enterprises for training, evaluation, and related work. He co-founded the company in 2023 with high-school friends Adarsh Hiremath and Surya Midha. Public reporting has described rapid revenue scale and multi-billion-dollar private valuations that placed the co-founders among the youngest self-made billionaires on some 2025 lists.

Foody left Georgetown to build full-time and was named a Thiel Fellow with his co-founders. Mercor evolved from early remote-engineering matching into a major supplier of human expertise for frontier AI systems.
