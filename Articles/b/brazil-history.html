History of Brazil

Selected overview for FuturPedia reference.

Colonial Period

Portuguese colonization began in the 16th century. Brazil became the center of a large plantation and mining economy and received more enslaved Africans than any other single destination in the Americas. The Portuguese court transferred to Brazil in 1808.

Empire & Republic

Brazil became an independent empire in 1822 under Pedro I. The monarchy lasted until 1889. The First Republic, Vargas era, military rule (1964–1985), and the return to democracy followed. Brazil is the largest country in Latin America and a major emerging economy.
