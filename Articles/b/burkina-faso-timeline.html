Burkina Faso — Key Historical Timeline

Selected major dates for FuturPedia reference. Not exhaustive.
  • c. 11th–15th centuries — Emergence and consolidation of Mossi kingdoms
  • 1896–1901 — French conquest and establishment of control
  • 1919 — Creation of the colony of Upper Volta
  • 1932 — Dissolution and partition of Upper Volta
  • 1947 — Reconstitution of Upper Volta
  • 1958 — Autonomous republic within the French Community
  • 5 August 1960 — Independence as the Republic of Upper Volta (Maurice Yaméogo president)
  • 1966 — Military coup; Sangoulé Lamizana takes power
  • 1980 — Coup led by Saye Zerbo
  • 1982 — Coup led by Jean-Baptiste Ouédraogo
  • 4 August 1983 — Thomas Sankara comes to power
  • 1984 — Country renamed Burkina Faso
  • 15 October 1987 — Assassination of Thomas Sankara; Blaise Compaoré takes power
  • 1991 — New constitution; multiparty framework under Compaoré
  • October–November 2014 — Popular uprising; resignation of Blaise Compaoré
  • 2022 — Two military coups
