Bitcoin (BTC)

Bitcoin is the first and largest decentralized digital currency. It was introduced in 2008 by an individual or group using the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto and launched in 2009.

Bitcoin operates on a peer-to-peer network without a central authority. Transactions are recorded on a public ledger called the blockchain, secured by proof-of-work mining. The total supply is capped at 21 million coins.

Bitcoin is often described as “digital gold” due to its fixed supply and use as a store of value, though it is also used as a medium of exchange. It remains the most widely recognized and highest-market-capitalization cryptocurrency.
