History of the United Kingdom

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Formation of the UK

The United Kingdom emerged from the union of England (and Wales) with Scotland (1707) and later Ireland (1801; most of Ireland left in 1922). England itself had a long history of monarchy, Parliament, and gradual constitutional development.

Empire & Industrial Revolution

Britain became the leading industrial and imperial power of the 19th century, with a vast global empire and pioneering role in industrialization.

20th Century & After

Two world wars, the creation of the welfare state, decolonization, membership in and later departure from the European Union, and continuing evolution of the constitutional monarchy and parliamentary system.
