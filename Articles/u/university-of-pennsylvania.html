University of Pennsylvania

Also known as: Penn; UPenn
Location: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, United States
Type: Private research university
Founded: 1740 (as a charity school; university status later)

The University of Pennsylvania is an Ivy League research university in Philadelphia. It is known for a broad set of undergraduate and graduate schools, including the Wharton School (business), the Perelman School of Medicine, the School of Engineering and Applied Science, the Law School, and strong programs across the arts and sciences.

Penn’s institutional identity mixes colonial-era origins associated with Benjamin Franklin’s educational ideas with a modern research university structure. It is a major producer of research, professional degrees, and alumni across business, medicine, law, and technology.
