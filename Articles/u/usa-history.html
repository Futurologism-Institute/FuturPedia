History of the United States

Highly condensed overview for FuturPedia reference.

Colonial Period & Independence

British colonies on the Atlantic coast developed distinct societies. The American Revolution (1775–1783) and the Constitution of 1787 created the United States as an independent federal republic.

19th Century

Territorial expansion, the Civil War (1861–1865) and the end of slavery, industrialization, and the rise of the United States as a continental and then global power.

20th & 21st Centuries

Two world wars, the Cold War, the civil rights movement, and the United States’ role as a leading economic, military, and cultural power. Ongoing debates about democracy, inequality, and global engagement continue to shape the country.
