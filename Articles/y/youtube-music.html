YouTube Music

Operator: Google (YouTube)
Type: Music and music-video streaming; free tier with ads and Premium tier
Relation: Built on YouTube’s catalogue and recommendation systems; successor path to Google Play Music

YouTube Music is Google’s music streaming product, drawing on YouTube’s official releases, user uploads, and recommendation stack. It competes with Spotify and Apple Music, with a large advantage in music-video and informal upload inventory.

Autoplay, mixes, and radio-style queues generate continuous listening from a seed track or taste profile—platform-controlled discovery rather than arbitrary external links.
