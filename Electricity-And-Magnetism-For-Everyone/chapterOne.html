Let’s watch the invisible rivers of electricity and magnetism! 1. What is Electromagnetism? Electromagnetism is the study of two invisible rivers that always travel together: • The electric river (we call it E) • The magnetic river (we call it B) These rivers live inside a big repository we call “the field repo.” In FuturPedia Theory, when we say something “exists,” we mean it exists as an entry inside this repo. 2. Maxwell’s Four Rules (said in friendly words) Maxwell found four rules that the rivers always obey: 1 Electric rivers start from electric charges. 2 Magnetic rivers never start or end — they always make closed loops. 3 A changing magnetic river creates an electric river that curls around it. 4 An electric current, or a changing electric river, creates a magnetic river that curls around it. 3. Writing the rules with FuturPedia logic We now write Maxwell’s equations using our careful symbols. Remember: ∃ means “there exists an entry inside the field repo.” Rule 1 – Gauss’s Law for Electricity ∀ regions R in the repo, the total electric river flowing out of R equals the total charge inside R (divided by a friendly constant). In symbols: ∀R ( ∇ · E(R) = ρ(R)/ε₀ ) Rule 2 – Gauss’s Law for Magnetism ᗑR (the magnetic river starts or ends inside R) which we write more strongly as: ∀R ( ∇ · B(R) = 0 ) There is never a magnetic charge entry in the repo. Rule 3 – Faraday’s Law ∀ loops L in the repo, the curling of the electric river around L equals the negative rate of change of the magnetic river through L. ∀L ( ∇ × E(L) = – ∂B/∂t (L) ) Rule 4 – Ampère–Maxwell Law ∀ loops L in the repo, the curling of the magnetic river around L equals the electric current through L plus the change of the electric river through L. ∀L ( ∇ × B(L) = μ₀ J(L) + μ₀ ε₀ ∂E/∂t (L) ) 4. Short FuturPedia-style proofs using the Wenitte operator Proof sketch of Rule 2 (no magnetic monopoles) Assume, for the sake of careful checking, that a magnetic charge exists somewhere. Then ∃R such that ∇ · B(R) ≠ 0. But every experiment recorded in the repo shows ∇ · B = 0. Therefore we have ᗑR (∇ · B(R) ≠ 0). By the Wenitte Lemma we do not need to point to a counter-example; it is enough to know that “not for all regions is the divergence non-zero.” Hence ∀R (∇ · B(R) = 0). Proof sketch of Rule 1 (electric charges source the field) Inside the repo we do find charge entries. Whenever ∃ charge in region R, we also find ∇ · E(R) = ρ(R)/ε₀. When ᗑ charge in R, the outgoing electric river is zero. Thus the universal rule holds: ∀R (∇ · E(R) = ρ(R)/ε₀). Proof sketch of Rules 3 and 4 (the curling rules) Grok and Wenitte observed that every changing river entry in the repo is always accompanied by a curling river of the other kind. No counter-example has ever been written into the repo. Therefore ᗑ (changing river without a curl). By FuturPedia reading of the quantifiers, the universal curling laws hold for all loops. 5.
