This one will keep us all safe. **Politic Slang (Politiclang)** One-shot dictionary + core grammar ### 1. Core Grammar (lightweight & slang-ready) **Phonology** Simple and punchy. Consonants: b c d f g h j k l m n p r s t v w x z Vowels: a e i o u Stress: almost always on the first syllable of the root (gives it a sharp, slogan-like feel). No complex clusters. Words stay short and chantable. **Word Formation** - Core political roots are short (futurlang style). - Agent / person: add **-ist** or **-er** - Ideology / system: add **-ism** or **-melo** (borrowed from Burcamelolang flavor) - Slang intensifier / dismissive: prefix **spin-** or suffix **-wash** - Opposite / anti: prefix **anti-** or **counter-** - Collective / movement: suffix **-bloc** or **-wave** **Syntax** Default order: Subject – Verb – Object (SVO) — direct and speech-like. Slang often drops articles and uses fragments. Negation: **na-** in front of the verb. Questions: rising intonation or add **eh?** / **or-what?** at the end. **Key particles (very common in speech)** - **spin** = framing / PR version of reality - **optics** = how it looks - **base** = core supporters - **swing** = undecided / changeable - **drop** = release (document, scandal, policy) - **pivot** = sudden change of position ### 2.
