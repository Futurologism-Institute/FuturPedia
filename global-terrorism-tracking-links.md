# Websites Tracking Global Terrorism

A curated list of major open-source databases, indices, and portals that track terrorist incidents, groups, and trends worldwide.

## Primary Databases & Incident Trackers

- **[Global Terrorism Database (GTD)](https://www.start.umd.edu/data-tools/GTD)**  
  Maintained by the National Consortium for the Study of Terrorism and Responses to Terrorism (START) at the University of Maryland. The most comprehensive unclassified database of terrorist attacks worldwide (1970–present), with over 200,000 incidents. Includes date, location, weapons, targets, casualties, and perpetrators.

- **[GTTAC Data Portal](https://gttac.com/data/)**  
  Global Terrorism Trends and Analysis Center. Interactive portal for querying, visualizing, and downloading open-source terrorism incident data with filters by region, tactics, weapons, perpetrators, and more.

- **[TRAC – Terrorism Research & Analysis Consortium](https://trackingterrorism.org/)**  
  Large database of terrorist/extremist group profiles, incident reports, videos, and real-time monitoring of IED/bombing incidents and online extremist activity.

- **[TerrorismTracker (Dragonfly Intelligence)](https://dragonflyintelligence.com/intelligence/terrorismtracker/)**  
  Comprehensive, daily-updated database of every terrorist incident and plot reported in open sources since 2007. Used as the primary data source for the Global Terrorism Index.

## Indices & Analytical Reports

- **[Global Terrorism Index (GTI)](https://www.visionofhumanity.org/maps/global-terrorism-index/)**  
  Produced by the Institute for Economics & Peace. Annual ranking and analysis of the impact of terrorism across 163 countries, based on incidents, fatalities, injuries, and hostages.

- **[Our World in Data – Terrorist Attacks](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/terrorist-attacks)**  
  Interactive charts and downloadable data derived primarily from the GTD, covering long-term trends in the number of terrorist attacks globally.

- **[Fondapol – Islamist Terrorist Attacks in the World (1979–2024)](https://www.fondapol.org/en/study/islamist-terrorist-attacks-in-the-world-1979-2024/)**  
  Detailed study and dataset focused specifically on Islamist terrorist attacks worldwide, with statistics on incidents, deaths, regions, and groups.
