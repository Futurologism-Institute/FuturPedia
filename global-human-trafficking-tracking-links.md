# Websites Tracking Global Human Trafficking

A curated list of major data hubs, reports, and portals that collect and publish statistics and case data on human trafficking and modern slavery worldwide.

## Primary Data Hubs & Datasets

- **[Counter Trafficking Data Collaborative (CTDC)](https://www.ctdatacollaborative.org/)**  
  The first global data hub on human trafficking. Aggregates de-identified individual-level case data from organizations worldwide (IOM, Polaris, and others). Includes interactive dashboards, maps, and downloadable synthetic/k-anonymized datasets covering 200,000+ victims across nearly 200 countries.

- **[UNODC Global Report on Trafficking in Persons (GLOTIP)](https://www.unodc.org/unodc/en/data-and-analysis/glotip.html)**  
  Official United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime biennial/global reports analyzing trafficking patterns, flows, victim profiles, and criminal justice responses. Latest editions cover data from 150+ countries.

- **[UNODC Data Portal – Trafficking in Persons](https://data.unodc.org/)**  
  Official statistical datasets on detected victims, offences, prosecutions, and convictions for trafficking in persons, disaggregated by sex, age, form of exploitation, and country.

## Government & Institutional Reports

- **[U.S. Department of State – Trafficking in Persons (TIP) Report](https://www.state.gov/reports/2024-trafficking-in-persons-report)**  
  Annual country-by-country assessment of governments’ efforts to combat trafficking, including tier rankings, narrative analyses, and estimates of the scale of the problem.

- **[Global Trafficking Intelligence Hub](https://globaltrafficking.org/global.html)**  
  Snapshot of key global estimates (modern slavery, forced labour, child labour) with links to primary sources (ILO, Walk Free, IOM, UNODC) and live data portals.

## Additional Visualization & Analysis Sources

- **[Our World in Data – Human Trafficking Victims](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/human-trafficking-victims)**  
  Charts and data on detected victims of human trafficking by age and sex, sourced from UNODC.

- **[ILO Forced Labour Observatory](https://www.ilo.org/topics/forced-labour-modern-slavery-and-trafficking-persons)**  
  International Labour Organization resources tracking legal frameworks, enforcement, and statistics related to forced labour and trafficking.
